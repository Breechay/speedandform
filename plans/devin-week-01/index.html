06

Two beefs

Split the 5 lb. One cook, 20 minutes each

LUNCH · KOREAN BEEF · 2.5 LB

Brown the beef with 5 cloves of minced garlic. Drain. Stir in 1/2 cup low-sodium soy sauce, 2 tbsp honey, 1 tbsp sesame oil, 1 tsp ground ginger and a pinch of red pepper flakes. Simmer 2 minutes. Top with green onion and sesame seeds.

Adapted from The Recipe Critic · less sugar

DINNER · SPICED BEEF · 2.5 LB

Hot pan, 1 tbsp olive oil. Break up the beef and cook until the water is gone. Add 1 tbsp cumin, 1 tbsp paprika, 1 tsp coriander, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1/2 tsp cinnamon and 1 tsp salt. Spread it flat and let it crisp. Finish with 1 tbsp tomato paste.

Adapted from Hungry Paprikas · less oil

Salad: cucumber, tomato, lemon, salt. Yogurt sauce: 1/2 cup Greek yogurt, lemon, 1 grated garlic clove, salt. Rice in one pot, broccoli steamed. Twelve containers: six Korean, six spiced.
