The study in 15 seconds.

We are not trying to build every part of his fitness before November. We are trying to find the fastest half-marathon pace he can actually carry.

Theory

The limiter now is not “more speed everywhere.”

Elijah has evidence of speed, but a 1:20 half is not proven. With six weeks left, the useful work is specific: make the pace more continuous, keep the rest recoverable, then taper.

Progression
3 × 2 mi → 2 × 3 mi → 3 × 2 mi → 6 mi continuousThen reduce the work and race.
Main test · Oct 27

Six miles continuous.

If he can hold 6:04–6:08 per mile evenly, with controlled breathing and legs, and recover normally, that pace stays in the race discussion.

If the run is too costly, the race pace comes down. The workout does not guarantee 13.1 miles.
