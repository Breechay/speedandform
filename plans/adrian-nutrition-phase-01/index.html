01 / Your day

One routine to repeat.

Built around your 8–5 workday. Keep lunch at work and dinner at home. These portions are your starting routine, not a limit on how much you may eat.

Beforeyour run

One banana.

Eat 1 medium banana about 20–30 minutes before your morning run. Keep the milk, peanut butter and larger breakfast for afterward.

On a day without a run, eat the banana with breakfast. Tell Brice when food before a run bothers your stomach; do not force it.

Aftertraining

Bread, peanut butter, orange. One shake.

Eat 2 slices of your mom’s bread, about 80 g combined, with 2 level tablespoons of peanut butter (32 g). Add 1 medium navel orange.

Shake 1 labeled serving of Thorne Whey Protein Isolate, Chocolate with 12 fl oz of whole milk (1½ cups / 355 mL). Drink it with breakfast.

Eat after your morning session, before work. When the morning runs tight, bring the breakfast with you and eat when you arrive. Do not wait until lunch.

12:30 p.m.work lunch

Eat the lunch work provides.

Keep the chicken with rice and beans, Cuban sandwich or other supplied lunch. Eat a full meal, not only the meat.

Send Brice photos of 2 normal work lunches. We will check the portions without asking you to replace a meal that is already provided.

3:30 p.m.prepared meal

Spaghetti with meat sauce.

Eat 1 of your 4 prepared boxes. Put it in the work fridge when you arrive. Reheat it in the microwave before eating.

Keep dinner. When this box repeatedly leaves you too full for dinner, tell Brice so we can reduce the box rather than quietly losing another meal.

6:30 p.m.family dinner

Your mom’s dinner, with a full plate.

Keep the chicken fricassee, beef, elk or other family main. Start with about 4–6 oz of cooked meat, 1½ cups of cooked rice and ½ cup of beans. Add 1 cup of cooked green beans.

Those rice-and-bean portions are a guide for that dinner, not extras to stack onto a full pasta dinner. Keep the family menu. Send 2 dinner photos so we can fit the portions to what is actually served.

Long-run fuel is separate.

Your running coach’s practiced fueling plan comes first. For your planned 12-mile run, roughly 90–102 minutes, discuss 30–60 g of carbohydrate per hour using the gels or drink you already tolerate. The banana alone is not the full long-run plan. Arrange water access and drink to thirst; do not force large amounts. Eat breakfast afterward. Sources 2, 8

This week: prepare breakfast, eat the afternoon box, and move your phone away before bed. Repeat these before adding more rules.

02 / Prepare ahead

Four pasta boxes.

16 oz / 454 g
Dry spaghetti
16 oz / 454 g
Raw 93% lean ground beef
24 oz / 680 g
Jarred tomato pasta sauce
  1. Cook the spaghetti according to the package. Drain it.
  2. Brown the beef in a pan. Check the center reaches 160°F with a food thermometer.
  3. Add the sauce, then combine with the pasta.
  4. Divide the finished batch equally among 4 shallow containers. Label the preparation date.

The pasta weight is dry. The beef weight is raw. Each box is one quarter of the finished batch, not 4 oz of cooked pasta.

This is a meal, not a tiny snack. No exact calories or protein per box are claimed without your ingredient labels.

Sunday and Wednesday.

Make one 4-box batch each time. On Sunday, refrigerate 3 boxes for Monday–Wednesday and freeze the fourth as your spare. On Wednesday, refrigerate 3 for Thursday–Saturday and freeze 1 for Sunday. Move Sunday’s box to the fridge on Saturday night.

You finish with 7 meals and 1 frozen backup. Carry chilled meals in an insulated bag with ice packs, then use your work fridge.

Cool it promptly. Reheat it fully.

Refrigerate the shallow containers within 2 hours, or within 1 hour in temperatures above 90°F. Keep the fridge at 40°F or below. Use refrigerated leftovers within 3–4 days. Freeze later portions. Cover, stir and reheat to 165°F throughout. Do not leave the food cooling on the counter for hours. Sources 4–5

03 / Close the day

Your phone gets a bedtime.

9:00 p.m.Phone away.
9:30 p.m.Lights out.

These are the targets. Charge the phone away from your bed. Move lights-out 30 minutes earlier every 3 nights until you reach 9:30.

At a 5:30 a.m. wake-up, that allows 8 hours in bed, not necessarily 8 hours asleep. The earlier bedtimes are steps toward enough sleep, not a reason to keep adding early sessions. Source 3

Missed prep or an evening training day?

No prepared box: pack 2 slices of bread with 2 tablespoons of peanut butter and 1 tablespoon of jam. Drink 8 fl oz of whole milk with it. Resume the pasta routine at the next planned meal. This is a practical backup, not an exact nutritional substitute.

Evening session: the larger pasta portion may need to move earlier. Keep the same food for the day, but ask Brice to place it around your actual start time. Do not force a full meal immediately before running.

Rest day: keep breakfast, lunch, your afternoon meal and dinner. Eat the banana with breakfast. Do not remove meals automatically because you did not run.

Your check-in · what we will adjust

After 7 days, send Brice a short note: breakfasts eaten, afternoon meals eaten, usual bedtime, appetite, digestion, soreness and how your runs felt. Send 2 lunch photos and 2 dinner photos during that week.

Use 3–4 morning weigh-ins per week, after the bathroom and before food, under the same conditions. We use the weekly average. There is no need to weigh yourself several times a day.

Protein guide: about 130–145 g each day from all food and the shake, based on your reported 156 lb / 70.8 kg. This is a guide, not a ceiling or a reason to crowd out bread, rice and pasta. Your lunch and dinner portions still need review. Source 1

No exact calorie surplus, deadline or final body weight has been set. We first check whether you follow the routine and tolerate it, then review food portions and your weight trend over 2 weeks. More muscle is the goal; more fatigue is not proof of progress.

Tell Brice before progressing a lift when soreness limits normal movement. Severe or worsening pain, marked weakness, dark urine or feeling unwell needs prompt medical assessment. Do not try to solve that with another shake. Source 9
