Three meals worth repeating.

Start with 01. Next cook, try 02, then 03. Then repeat your favorite. One recipe per batch.

01 · Start here

Spaghetti with meat sauce.

Soften onion, brown beef, build a real tomato sauce.

Cook Recipe 01

Finish: ¼ cup / 20 g grated Parmesan (half batch: 2 tbsp / 10 g).

  1. Make the sauce.
  2. Boil the spaghetti. Save a mug of pasta water.
  3. Back in the pot: sauce and Parmesan. Toss 1 minute on low. Too dry? Pasta water, 1 tbsp at a time.
  4. Divide into 4 boxes (half batch: 2). Label. Into the fridge.

Cue: the sauce clings. No puddle underneath.

02 · Second cook

Creamy tomato penne.

Same beef base, softer sauce.

Cook Recipe 02

Finish: ½ cup / 120 mL heavy cream (half batch: ¼ cup / 60 mL).

  1. Make the sauce. Boil the penne. Save a mug of pasta water.
  2. Sauce off the heat. Stir in the cream. Back on low until hot. Don't boil.
  3. Combine with the penne. Loosen with pasta water if needed.
  4. Divide into 4 boxes (half batch: 2). Label. Into the fridge.

Cue: smooth and pink-orange.

03 · Third cook

Cheesy beef pasta.

Finish a sauce with cheese, not more heat.

Cook Recipe 03

Finish: 1 cup / 112 g shredded cheddar (half batch: ½ cup / 56 g).

  1. Make the sauce. Boil the penne. Save a mug of pasta water.
  2. Combine pasta and sauce. Turn off the heat.
  3. Add cheddar in 3 handfuls, stirring until each melts. Won't melt? Low heat briefly. Don't boil.
  4. Divide into 4 boxes (half batch: 2). Label. Into the fridge.

Cue: glossy, not oily or grainy.

Keep your food safe
  • Into the fridge within 2 hours of cooking, or 1 hour above 90°F. Fridge at 40°F or below.
  • A microwave is not cold storage. Cooked food sitting there counts as time out. More than 2 hours at room temperature: discard it.
  • Cooked boxes last 3 to 4 days in the fridge. Freeze anything for later.
  • Raw ground beef lasts 1 to 2 days in the fridge. Thaw it in the fridge on a tray, never on the counter.
  • Beef cooks to 160°F. A reheated box reaches 165°F in the center. Check with the thermometer, never inside a running microwave.
  • To reheat: add 1 tbsp water, vent the lid, heat, stir, heat again. Open the lid away from your face.
  • Wash your hands and anything raw beef touched. Don't rinse the meat.
  • A thawed box is for the next day. Don't change its label date.

When a meal falls through
  • Box still frozen: defrost it in the microwave, then reheat to 165°F. Never on the counter.
  • No box: 2 slices bread, 2 tbsp peanut butter, 1 tbsp jam, 8 fl oz whole milk. It replaces the missing meal, not breakfast. No extra scoop.
  • No milk with you: eat the sandwich and buy 8 fl oz of milk.
  • Missed Wednesday cook: the frozen backup Thursday, the sandwich Friday. Don't eat Sunday's boxes on Friday.
  • Evening session: the 3:30 box comes first. If you missed it and you are already very hungry, eat the backup sandwich and drink water before training. Home from work: start training within 30 minutes. Dinner right after.
  • Rest day: keep every meal.
