Half marathon · Sub-1:30

Race Pace Durability

Sub-1:30 is not really a pace problem. Most runners who want it can already touch 6:52 a mile. The question is whether it is still there at mile eleven. So the key sessions are run at 6:30–6:45, faster than goal pace, to build the margin that makes race day hold.
