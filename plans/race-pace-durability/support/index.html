A few things to know

Is this the right starting point?

If six running days, about 45 miles a week and a 12-mile long run are normal for you, start by trying Weeks 1–4. This version builds toward about 60 miles a week and an 18-mile long run. It is a demanding half-marathon build.

How do I choose my pace?

Begin with a race-pace range and threshold pace supported by your recent races or training. The plan does not calculate those for you. If you are unsure what fits, ask about Run Development before starting.

Does the $79 include coaching or the app?

Your one-time payment covers the complete 15-week web plan and print editions. Individual coaching and FORM app access are separate. There is no subscription required to use the plan.

Can I use it on another device?

Yes. Restore access with the same email used at checkout. We verify that email before opening the paid weeks. If you need help, email Brice.
