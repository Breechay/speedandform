About this plan

Race Pace Durability is a free 15-week half marathon plan for experienced runners targeting sub-1:30.

It starts around 45 miles per week, peaks near 60, and builds the ability to carry race pace late into the race.

Before you start

This is not a beginner half marathon plan.

You should already be comfortable training six days per week, handling roughly 45 miles in the opening week, and completing a 12-mile long run without it becoming the main event of the week.

Sub-1:30 pace

A 1:29:59 half marathon averages about 6:52 per mile / 4:16 per kilometer.

The plan’s key sessions are authored at 6:30–6:45 per mile on purpose — faster than goal pace to build enough durability and margin to make sub-1:30 sustainable on race day.
