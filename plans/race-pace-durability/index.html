About this plan

Build the ability to carry the pace.

Race Pace Durability is a 15-week half-marathon plan for runners who are already training consistently.

Early sessions break the race-pace work into repeatable pieces. Key weeks remove the reset and extend the continuous distance. Later long runs ask you to find the pace again after earlier miles.

The progression

The target does not simply get faster every week.

Shorter efforts → 5 continuous miles → 6 → 8 → 12 race-pace miles after 4 easier miles → race.

The build and absorb weeks between those asks are part of the plan, not empty space.

Before you start

This is not a beginner plan.

You should already be comfortable running about 45 miles per week, training six days per week, and completing a 12-mile long run.

Use a pace your current running supports

Recent races and workouts should support the pace you choose. Faster and slower runners can use the same progression at different paces.

Hope is currently using 6:45–7:00/mi and José 6:30–6:45/mi. Those are their assigned paces, not a requirement for the plan.
