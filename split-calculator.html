Distance and goal time to watch-ready splits.

Choose a distance and finish time to see average pace and even-pace checkpoints. The numbers describe that finish time; they do not assess your fitness or set your training paces.

Use whole minutes (90), minutes:seconds (90:00), or hours:minutes:seconds (1:30:00). After a colon, use two digits from 00 to 59.

Use a positive number, such as 8 or 8.5. Choose kilometers or miles beside it.
