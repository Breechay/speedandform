# RPD Creative B — Product Inspection / Trust

**Status:** build-ready spec, not yet created in Meta  
**Purpose:** Test a different reason to click than Creative A. A sells identity + problem recognition. B sells transparency + product inspection.

## Hypothesis

Some qualified runners do not need another emotional hook. They need to see that the $79 product is real, specific and runnable.

## Core line

> **SEE THE FIRST FOUR WEEKS.**

Plain alternatives worth letting Meta expand:

- `Try the first four weeks free.`
- `See the plan before you buy it.`
- `Start with four weeks free.`

Reject fake scarcity, `secret`, guarantees, personalization claims or wrong race distance.

## Visual

Use the actual RPD web plan / week view, not a stylized training diagram.

Show enough real detail to communicate:

- this is a real 15-week plan;
- there are six running days;
- workouts and long runs are specified;
- the opening four weeks are genuinely available before purchase.

Do not make the screenshot so zoomed out that no workout text can be read.

## Seed primary text

`Training for a faster half marathon? Start with the first four weeks of Race Pace Durability free. Follow the plan, see how it fits your training, then unlock the complete 15 weeks with a one-time payment of $79.`

Let Meta generate alternatives. Keep only truthful, immediately understandable variants.

## Headline seed

`Try Weeks 1–4 Free`

Other acceptable simple variants:

- `See the Plan Before You Buy`
- `15 Weeks of Half-Marathon Training`
- `Full Plan · One-Time Payment of $79`

## Description

`15 weeks · 6 days/week · first 4 weeks free`

## CTA

`Learn More` / Meta CTA enhancement allowed.

## Destination

English RPD offer page:
`https://speedandform.com/plans/race-pace-durability/support/`

URL tags:
`utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=rpd_purchase_test_01&utm_content=weeks_1_4_preview_v1`

Spanish translation may be enabled only while the meaningful Spanish destination flow remains supported.

## Difference from Creative A

**A:** real FORM people + `Can you keep the pace?` = identity / aspiration / felt problem.  
**B:** actual product + `See the first four weeks` = transparency / trust / concrete value.

Do not turn B into another group-photo copy variant. The experiment only teaches us something if the buying reason is genuinely different.
