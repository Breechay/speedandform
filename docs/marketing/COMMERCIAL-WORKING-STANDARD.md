## What a visitor needs to understand

A relevant page should quickly answer: Is this for me? What will I receive? Why trust Brice with this? What does it cost, including important exclusions? What happens if I inquire? What do I do next?

Use a clear service/location label, an outcome in ordinary language, real representative work and one primary next action. Personality belongs in the voice, photography and point of view. Avoid making the visitor decode internal product language. Prices and exclusions should help people choose, not appear only after they surrender contact details. Keep forms limited to information needed for the first useful reply.

Warm introductions and cold search have different starting points. Someone who met Brice may already trust him and needs the correct offer link, scope and follow-through. A new search visitor needs more explicit identity, relevant evidence and practical details. Neither path should require an account to evaluate a service. Use the same canonical offer page; do not duplicate near-identical pages for each neighborhood.

## Research basis and limits

- [Google people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content): build useful, original material for an intended audience. SEO supports it; no ranking or revenue guarantee follows.
- [NN/g information scent](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/information-scent/): link labels, surrounding context and images help visitors judge relevance. Apply descriptive service/navigation labels and representative images.
- [NN/g trustworthy design](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/trustworthy-design/): understandable costs, practical information and proportionate information requests support credibility. Avoid hidden costs and unnecessary gates.
- [Google purchase-decision research](https://business.google.com/en-all/think/consumer-insights/navigating-purchase-behavior-and-decision-making/): exploration and evaluation may repeat. This is useful context for discoverability and reassurance, not a validated conversion formula for Miami coaching.

Reviewed September 30. These include established research, not a claim that the newest article is best. Recommendations for these specific pages remain hypotheses until observed with relevant buyers. Do not copy ecommerce effect sizes into service forecasts. No fake scarcity, testimonials, savings, medical claims, exaggerated authority or guaranteed transformation.

## Order of work and definition of done

1. **Reliability:** real page, correct CTA, accessible mobile layout, accepted-only inquiry receipt, truthful payment/delivery path. Fix reproducible defects before promotion.
2. **Evidence and economics:** small real samples, representative demos, scope, availability, total delivery hours and costs. Brice supplies judgment, selection and capacity; agents assemble, test and document.
3. **Distribution:** share the relevant service page in real conversations, referrals and purposeful introductions. Record channel and actual inquiry outcome without personal information in analytics.
4. **Learning:** weekly review of qualified exposure, accepted inquiries, conversations, bookings, payment, delivery, repeat/referral and enjoyment. Unknown is not zero. Compare paid and unpaid separately. Do not call a lightly exposed offer rejected.
5. **Refinement:** one meaningful change/hypothesis at a time per offer. At low traffic, record buyer questions and usability failures; do not pretend a small A/B test establishes superiority. Visual polish serves comprehension and trust.

Every task has an owner, one next action, finish condition, evidence and review date where needed. Completion requires observation or Brice's report.
