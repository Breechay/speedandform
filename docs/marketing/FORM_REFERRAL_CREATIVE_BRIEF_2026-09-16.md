# FORM referral + offer creative brief

**Version:** 3.0  
**Date:** September 16, 2026  
**Destination:** speedandform.com  
**Market:** Miami  
**Purpose:** self-contained brief for independent creative, growth, brand, coaching and conversion review

Reviewers should not assume access to prior conversations. Challenge the proposed system rather than merely polishing it.

## Assignment

Design and critique a small paid/organic creative system for FORM that turns existing referral behavior and useful coaching content into qualified demand.

This is no longer a one-ad problem. There are two legitimate coaching jobs and three distinct creative mechanisms to test.

Central question:

> How can FORM create running content that the right runner recognizes as useful, and that another person naturally wants to send to a runner they know, while routing each runner to the appropriate depth of help?

## The two offers

### FORM Analysis — working product, scope/price still open

**Job:** `Understand your running.`

For a runner who already has a training structure or has a specific running concern and does not necessarily want FORM to take over their plan.

Working shape: running observation, video/movement review, athlete report, optional software-assisted analysis, 1–3 priorities, cues/drills, practice/retest, concise take-home findings. One vs two contacts and exact deliverable are open.

Not the promise: perfect form, universal biomechanics, injury diagnosis/prevention, or fixing every visible difference.

Doctrine:

> **Not everything needs fixing. Find the few things worth changing.**

Ochy or similar software may be used as an instrument. The software score is not the product and must not be presented as an objective grade of the runner. FORM sells judgment about what matters and what to do.

**Pricing is open.** Do not divide the $1,200 managed block by eight. A concentrated specialist encounter can rationally have a higher per-contact price. Working research band only: $250–$400; approximately $325 is a test anchor if scope includes meaningful review/artifact/retest. Agents must challenge scope and economics before recommending public pricing.

### Run Development — current flagship managed coaching

**Job:** `Develop your running.`

Current structure: **8 weeks · $1,200**.

Assessment → individual running plan → mostly easy practice/consistency → appropriate speed/threshold work → endurance → feedback → adjustment.

The point is not to hand the athlete workouts. It is to develop the runner.

### They must not compete

Do not create a menu of one, two, three and eight sessions. Self-sort by job:

| | FORM Analysis | Run Development |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Question | What is happening when I run? | Can you develop my running? |
| Scope | Observe → prioritize → cue → retest | Assess → plan → coach → adjust → develop |
| Plan | Athlete may keep existing plan/coach | FORM owns running-development process |
| Commitment | Bounded specialist encounter | 8-week relationship |

A major unresolved product issue is the current complimentary first Run Development track assessment. Before a paid Analysis product launches, define why that free assessment is not the same service sold separately.

## Observed acquisition evidence

### Referral behavior

A trainer at House of Transformation has repeatedly introduced runners to FORM when they need running help. Referred needs include:
- an Ironman 70.3 trainee who wants help with running form while already living inside a broader triathlon context;
- a newer half-marathon runner looking for someone to help with running;
- a runner preparing to lead a run club and wanting hands-on coaching;
- runners described simply as needing a running coach.

After FORM explained the Run Development approach to one referral, she responded: **“This is exactly what I’m looking for.”**

The pattern is not that prospects arrive asking for a named package. They arrive saying, in effect:

> **I run, and I want someone to help me become better at running.**

The Ironman referral adds a second pattern:

> **I already have training, but something about my running needs attention.**

That is the product reason for FORM Analysis.

### Useful-content behavior

A non-client sent unsolicited feedback after watching FORM's recent athlete videos. He described changing aspects of arm action and leg cycle from what he observed and reported that his run felt **easier / more efficient at the same pace**.

The important evidence is behavioral, not the compliment:

> **Watch → learn → try → perceive useful change.**

This supports the hypothesis that the strongest ad may be a genuinely useful FORM video that is then promoted.

## Acquisition paths

### A — Direct recognition
content/ad → `that is me` → site → appropriate offer → inquiry → encounter/client

### B — Referral recognition
content/ad → `I know who needs this` → send/share → runner → site → appropriate offer

### C — Value first
content → useful observation → try/save/follow → later inquiry or referral

Do not judge the campaign only on immediate lead count. Track qualified inquiries, paid conversion and referrals alongside attention/share signals.

## Three primary creative hypotheses

These are different mechanisms, not three edits of one commercial.

### Creative 1 — Recognition / Run Development

**Spine:**

# Run better.
# Get faster.
# Run farther.

Footage does the explaining.

**Run better:** full-body mechanics, Brice observing, authentic coaching interaction.  
**Get faster:** track rep, acceleration, timing, controlled hard running.  
**Run farther:** wider sustained running, continuation, composure.

Working rhythm for a 12–18s master:
- 0:00–0:04 Run better.
- 0:04–0:08 Get faster.
- 0:08–0:12 Run farther.
- 0:12–0:15/18 close.

Working close:

**RUN DEVELOPMENT**  
Individual running coaching · Miami  
**speedandform.com**

Hypothesis: broad recognition makes ongoing development desirable and remains easy to forward.

Open test: price in film vs ad copy vs landing page.

### Creative 2 — Coaching value / FORM Analysis

This should feel like a useful FORM post that happens to be promoted.

Possible structure:
1. runner moving;
2. one visible/experienced issue or question;
3. Brice observing;
4. one authentic priority/cue in context;
5. runner practicing/retesting;
6. selective analysis overlay or Ochy instrument shot only if it clarifies the observation;
7. close on FORM Analysis.

Possible closing language to test:

**FORM ANALYSIS**  
Understand your running.  
Miami · speedandform.com

Do not promise that a cue fixed pain, prevented injury, or universally improved efficiency. If the athlete's before/after is not genuinely demonstrated, do not manufacture one.

Hypothesis: demonstrating judgment before asking for a click creates save/share value and makes the bounded specialist product legible.

### Creative 3 — Referral native

Design for the viewer to think:

> **I know exactly who needs this.**

Do not default to `share this with a runner`, fake iMessage theater or generic engagement bait. Reproduce the transferability of a trusted recommendation.

The creative can begin from a recognizable runner problem rather than a product name. It may end at a two-door site handoff:

**FORM Analysis** — Understand your running.  
**Run Development** — Develop your running.

Hypothesis: FORM can digitally amplify the same behavior already producing paying clients through trusted field referrals.

## Audience

Do not reduce the audience to fast runners chasing PRs.

Relevant people include newer runners, race trainees, triathletes, athletes from other sports, run-club leaders, experienced runners, and people who want running to feel smoother or more capable.

Common denominator:

> **A person who cares enough about running to want to become a better runner.**

Secondary audience: the connector — trainer, friend, run-club member or athlete who hears someone say `I need help with my running.`

## Creative principles

- Real coaching and real athlete footage over staged advertising.
- One main idea on screen at a time.
- Movement must remain legible.
- Useful before clever.
- No generic motivational montage.
- No feature-list commercial.
- No montage consisting only of very fast runners.
- No fake before/after.
- No universal form hacks.
- No implication that every difference needs correction.
- No medical diagnosis from video-analysis software.
- No fabricated testimonials/results.
- The film must work muted.
- Natural footfalls, track ambience and one real coaching cue may be stronger than generic music.
- Paid-media audio must be commercially cleared.

Visual direction: restrained/editorial, vertical 9:16, black-and-white or restrained color when source footage supports it, readable typography continuous with speedandform.com, clean negative space, no decorative transitions or artificial speed ramps merely to create excitement.

## Website handoff

The website has historically worked as a trusted referral destination. Preserve that.

The site should let a visitor self-sort by need without becoming a giant pricing grid:

> **FORM Analysis**  
> Understand your running.

> **Run Development**  
> Develop your running.

Run Development remains the flagship managed service. Analysis is not `Run Development Lite`.

Review whether Analysis needs its own detail page and whether the homepage should introduce both doors. Do not build either until scope, price and the complimentary-assessment boundary are resolved.

## Organic + paid should be one system

**Paid:** creates recognition.  
**Organic:** repeatedly demonstrates how FORM sees/cues/develops runners.  
**Website:** explains which relationship fits.  
**Analysis/assessment:** lets the runner experience specific judgment.  
**Coaching:** produces development and future evidence/referrals.

The product should create the next acquisition evidence.

## What the first tests should teach

1. Does useful coaching outperform pure cinematography as an opening?
2. Is `Run better. Get faster. Run farther.` understood quickly?
3. Does a bounded Analysis offer capture people who would reject an 8-week relationship?
4. Does Analysis cannibalize Run Development or correctly segment a different job?
5. What exact concern language do Analysis prospects use?
6. Does showing an analysis instrument increase trust or make FORM look software-led?
7. Which creative earns meaningful sends/shares?
8. Does price placement improve qualification enough to offset reduced response?
9. Does the site preserve the trust of a personal referral?
10. What is the real cost to serve each offer?

## Success standard

The system should create one of three thoughts:

> **That would help me.**

> **I know who needs this.**

> **I want FORM to develop my running.**

Then the site should route the runner to the appropriate depth of help without making the two offers compete.

The goal is not advertising separated from FORM. The goal is to make **FORM itself more useful and more transferable.**
