## Shared commercial rule

Use Unbounce as an evidence-backed idea source, not as the brand voice authority.

For every concept reviewed:

- first ask whether a fit athlete understands it without coaching vocabulary;
- lead with outcome / felt problem, then what they get, then what they do;
- let real FORM people and results carry sophistication;
- preserve product truth and reject invented personalization, urgency, guarantees, scarcity or claims;
- generic copy is acceptable when it is clear, true and conversion-oriented;
- keep the production site visually FORM rather than copying template aesthetics literally.

## 1. RPD landing-page exploration

### Current ad story

The first paid visual uses a real FORM athlete group photo because it is one of FORM's strongest-engagement social assets. The visual sells people, fitness, identity, energy and proof-of-practice. The copy sells the plan.

Central question:

> **Can you keep the pace?**

The landing page should feel like the next frame after the ad, not a separate essay.

### Desired first-screen story

- same central question as the ad;
- use the real FORM group image high on the page when the clean asset is available;
- identify the image truthfully as FORM runners / FORM practice, without implying every pictured athlete personally runs RPD unless verified;
- plain description: 15-week half-marathon plan, used with FORM runners, designed to help runners hold race pace longer;
- show workload and price immediately: 15 weeks, 6 days/week, roughly 45→60 miles/week, one-time payment of $79;
- primary cold-traffic action: try Weeks 1–4 free;
- full-plan purchase remains visible as the secondary immediate action.

### Page sequence to test / refine

1. Ad continuity: same question, group proof, clear offer.
2. What the athlete wants: run a faster / stronger half marathon, hold pace longer.
3. What happens over 15 weeks: shorter race-pace efforts → 5 → 6 → 8 → 12 late → race.
4. Simple fit check: already running consistently; this is not a first-half-marathon base plan.
5. Real FORM evidence: Hope / José live use, Simon verified FORM result as broader coaching credibility where clearly distinguished.
6. What the buyer gets: every week, every workout, long runs, warm-up / recovery / pace guidance.
7. Price / purchase / recovery.
8. Deeper method only after the offer is already understood.

### Unbounce audit questions

Inspect templates, AI/copy tools and conversion guidance for:

- best hero structures for low-ticket digital products / fitness / coaching;
- image + copy proportions on mobile and desktop;
- CTA repetition frequency and placement;
- social proof above vs below first CTA;
- simple pricing blocks;
- preview/free-sample framing;
- qualification sections that do not feel like an application form;
- FAQ / objection order;
- sticky/mobile CTAs if supported;
- whether a long page or shorter direct-response page fits this $79 product better;
- patterns for ad-message → landing-page message match.

### RPD rejection rules

Reject any suggested copy that says or implies:

- marathon instead of half marathon;
- personalized / tailored individual programming when buying the fixed self-guided plan;
- guaranteed race time or finish result;
- "limited time" unless a real deadline exists;
- secret / hack / no more plateaus / instant transformation;
- simple monotonic intensity increase if that misstates the training;
- app subscription required to finish RPD.
