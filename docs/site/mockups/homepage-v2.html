The people

Two men. Opposite problems.

These are athletes from the same group. In 2025 we ran the Keys100 relay with twelve of them, across two teams of six.

Marcus Balliette

The goal
Break 1:30 in the half at West Palm Beach, December 2026.
The constraint
He had the speed. His threshold work was not calibrated well enough to hold it, and the heat made that worse. His long runs were not carrying race-pace miles where they mattered.
The decision
I moved his threshold range, added speed work to clean up his mechanics, and placed race-relevant miles deliberately inside the long runs.
Where it stands
Six weeks holding about 6:15 per mile at threshold. The attempt is still ahead of him.

Tinius Maximilian Seland

The goal
Broader shoulders and a sharper waist. He had spent years on it.
The constraint
Willingness was never the problem. What he had been following was too vague to tell him what his physique actually needed, and he executes exactly what he is given, almost robotically. Given more options, he does all of them.
The decision
I chose the areas that change the frame most, shoulders and midsection, and gave him limits instead of options. The midsection is why I put him on the track. The running was not a reward for finishing the strength work. It was part of the answer to the waist.
Where it stands
He has the frame and the waist. He also has a Garmin-estimated VO₂ max of 63, which he never asked for. He is currently taking a break from structured running and has put most of his attention back into FORGE.

Built close to the work.

Most people I coach are already carrying a full life. They may have a race date, a physique goal, or both, but not hours to sort through conflicting advice. You should not have to become your own coach before you can train well.

I have been writing these plans since 2017.

FORM is the group. Tuesday mornings at the track. Saturday long runs from Hideout in Edgewater, the café I co-own. The app is named after it. It holds the plans I used to hand out as PDFs, then put on this website.

The apps are where the work is kept now. Open the app. See today's work. Execute.
