# Speed & Form homepage — V5 positioning

## Locked hierarchy

The homepage shares one philosophy but sells two distinct outcomes. FORM and FORGE are not a forced pair and neither customer has to adopt the other discipline.

- **FORM:** race preparation, especially first and repeat half marathons. It turns the race goal into the week in front of the runner, sets the paces, and shows what belongs today.
- **FORGE:** a complete men’s physique program. It assigns more work to the areas that most change proportion and appearance while developing and maintaining the rest in balance. Strength is present in the work, but the buyer-facing promise is a better-built physique.
- **Shared belief:** scattered training advice becomes useful only when it is organized around the athlete, goal, schedule, completed work, sequence, and emphasis.
- **Optional ecosystem:** an athlete may eventually use both. This is a quiet possibility, not the main sale.

## Do not regress

- Do not restore a FORM / BUILD BOTH / FORGE rail.
- Do not make “hybrid athlete” or using both apps the homepage thesis.
- Do not reduce FORM to a generic running organizer. Race preparation is the sharp buyer story.
- Do not reduce FORGE to generic strength training. Physique, proportion, emphasis, and a complete program are the sharp buyer story.
- Keep cross-introductions small and optional.
