# Speed & Form Homepage — V6.1 integration fix

## What changed

- Fixed distortion of FORM and FORGE screenshots caused by intrinsic `width`/`height` attributes being combined with responsive CSS widths without `height:auto`.
- Moved the glass treatment from a rounded inner capsule to the full sticky navigation bar. This is more stable in Safari, feels integrated with the page, and removes the exposed strip around the glass.
- Added the original SF+ mark as a small adaptive symbol in the header and footer. It inherits the current navigation color and turns warm-white in the FORGE chapter.
- Added one restrained footer note explaining the name without interrupting the product conversion path.

## Brand ruling

The SF+ mark is a signature, not a hero illustration. Use it in navigation, footer, favicon/social details, or small physical applications. Do not enlarge it into a dominant motorsport-style graphic.

`Speed & Form` names the balance behind the work: restraint and fire, composure and intensity. That idea remains implicit through the product pages and is explained only once, quietly, in the footer.

## Typography ruling

- Cormorant Garamond remains reserved for the homepage promise and typography already inside product screenshots.
- Jost carries the website's explanatory and conversion voice.
- JetBrains Mono carries navigation, labels, metadata, and the Speed & Form lockup.
