# Speed & Form doctrine

For the next agent. Do not reverse this.

The site is one practice with four rooms.

- Practice: the week built around you, or a plan you follow yourself.
- Occasion: Thursday at the track. Saturday when one is scheduled. Do not promise a Saturday every week.
- Record: the study, the photographs, what the athletes actually did.
- Home: the door around the occasion. Optional. The other three already exist.

An athlete enters through practice or the occasion. A partner enters through home. Both should recognize the other three rooms.

## How a page should open

Desire, then the path, then one proof, then one next step. Named objects, not essays. If a page takes more than a few seconds to grasp, add an object. Do not add a paragraph.

The invitation is always: Tell me what you want to be capable of.

## What not to add

- A second homepage on a door page.
- A second telling of the price.
- Philosophy, gait essays, kite lines, or FAQ walls on the first screen.
- Claims we do not have. No race prediction. The September 29 six miles are a training milestone.
- "Most runners," "without the fades," or "felt good the next day."
- Generated lifestyle photographs. Empty frames until a real Arrival or Return photograph exists.
- A Saturday cadence we have not earned.
