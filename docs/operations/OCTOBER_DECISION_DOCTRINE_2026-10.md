# October Decision Doctrine
## Speed & Form · 2026-10-04

This is a decision filter, not another task database. The Operating Console and current roadmaps still own status and next actions. Newer verified source facts supersede this document.

## Executive ruling

**The problem is dilution, not lack of ideas.**

- Coaching is the commercial anchor.
- Plans distribute the method.
- Community is practice.
- Studies and photography are proof.
- FORM House is a setting, not a business.
- FORM and Forge are infrastructure.

October should not make Speed & Form larger. It should make the existing practice easier to understand, trust, buy and continue.

## October mandate

1. Protect existing customer delivery and make Run Development easier to enter and continue.
2. Make the existing $79 Race Pace That Lasts plan reliably understandable, purchasable and usable.
3. Produce one excellent, permissioned proof asset that demonstrates coaching judgment.

Everything else must earn its place by materially improving **delivery, trust, proof or revenue**.

## Active-work cap

Keep no more than **two bounded experiments** active alongside the core business.

### Experiment A · Proof film / photo story
One real training story. One question. One audience. The finished asset should help a viewer understand the quality of the practice, not merely look cinematic.

Suggested structure:
**Arrival → preparation → effort → human moment → return.**

Creative-service exploration continues only when:
- the finished work survives an outside critique;
- permissions/rights are clear;
- at least one plausible buyer can name a real use for work like it; and
- a bounded deliverable, revision limit and delivery date can be stated.

If the asset is useful to Speed & Form but no buyer has a defined use, approver or budget, keep it as proof. That is still a successful outcome.

### Experiment B · Hosted-morning discovery
Do not “launch FORM House.” First determine whether a named host has a guest-experience problem for which a small coach-led morning is a useful paid solution.

A pilot requires:
- named host / decision-maker;
- real access path and plausible date window;
- run scope and guest limit;
- food/service owner;
- staffing and media-permission plan;
- weather/cancellation plan;
- direct costs and Brice's time estimated from real information;
- a viable answer to who pays.

A well-received one-off proves delivery, not repeatable demand.

**Do not assume Hideout is an available pilot venue. It closed in August 2026.**

### Reactive opportunities
Photography/video commissions advance only when a named buyer has a real use and a scoped proposal path.

Runner-strength companion stays parked until the Oct 23 review window unless a real customer problem justifies earlier attention.

## The product is judgment

The valuable sequence is:

**Observe → decide → structure → review → adjust.**

At a meaningful review point, preserve:
**Goal · Constraint · Observation · Decision · Work completed · Outcome · Uncertainty · Next decision.**

A training milestone is not a race prediction. A reported feeling is not an objective measurement. One athlete improving does not prove that one intervention caused the result.

## Two commercial paths

### Run Development
**Recognized goal → qualified inquiry → fit conversation → clear recommendation → paid start → delivery → review → appropriate continuation**

A fit conversation is not an unpaid full assessment. Close with one best-fit recommendation.

### Race Pace That Lasts
**Discovery → suitability → preview → purchase → access → successful use**

Treat these as different questions:
- Is the product understandable?
- Is purchase reliable?
- Is first use reliable?
- Is there repeated demand?

Use status language precisely:
**Proposed → Prepared → Tested → Deployed → Customer-verified.**

Do not collapse these states into “done.”

## Website law

Hierarchy before information.

A commercial entry point should quickly answer:
1. Who is this for?
2. What problem does it help solve?
3. What will I receive?
4. What should I do next?

Do not make a visitor understand the entire ecosystem before understanding one offer.

## Workday rule

A normal workday has three finishes:

1. **Deliver** — finish something an existing customer depends on.
2. **Sell or learn** — move one real buyer/referral conversation or purchase blocker forward.
3. **Prove** — produce one useful proof asset, applied skill output or system repair.

A fourth outcome needs a real reason. Hats are thinking modes, not eight simultaneous task lists.

## Weekly decision rule

Every weekly review ends with exactly:
- **ONE CONTINUE** — what earned another week;
- **ONE STOP / PARK** — what no longer deserves active attention;
- **ONE OWNER ACTION** — Brice's next concrete action and due date.

Track conversations, clear offers, paid coaching starts, verified plan purchases, real delivery problems resolved and permissioned proof separately.

Do not invent a coaching conversion target without a baseline.
