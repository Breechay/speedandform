# FORM House · Partner Concept
## One hosted morning · October 2026

**FORM House is a setting for the practice, not a venue business.**

The proposed unit is one carefully hosted morning that begins with outdoor training and ends with people feeling looked after.

## The idea

**Different runs. One breakfast table.**

A small group arrives early. Runners choose the route or training lane that fits the day. The groups return into a prepared environment: belongings handled, water and towels ready, good food and coffee, comfortable seating and enough time to stay.

The run matters. The quality of the return matters just as much.

## What FORM brings

- authored route / training structure;
- clear communication before arrival;
- a named run leader for each departure;
- an inclusive model where different abilities and prescriptions remain valid;
- thoughtful hosting around the training morning;
- optional, separately permissioned photography when useful.

## What a host may bring

- early access;
- secure belongings;
- bathrooms / changing / showers when available;
- hospitality staffing;
- food and drinks;
- seating and climate relief;
- any agreed recovery or guest amenities.

No amenity is implied merely because the property has it.

## What makes this different

This is not a proposal to add another generic run club.

The relevant question is:

> **Does the property have a guest or resident experience that becomes better when the whole training morning is handled thoughtfully?**

A longer-run format, multiple valid training lanes, a genuine breakfast window, a place to remain and a strong visual record can be combined only when those elements solve a real host need.

## First conversation

Before proposing a date, establish:

- Who owns the decision?
- What does the property already offer?
- What guest/resident problem remains unsolved?
- Which part of this concept is actually useful here?
- Who pays?
- What access can be guaranteed?
- What guest count can the property genuinely support?

## Pilot threshold

A pilot exists only when:
- a named decision-maker wants to explore it;
- a date window and access path are realistic;
- food/service ownership is clear;
- direct costs and FORM's time can be estimated;
- guest capacity is known; and
- the economics do not depend on unrealistic attendance.

One enjoyable morning is evidence that the experience can be delivered. It is not proof of recurring demand.

## Public-facing line

**Come for the miles. Stay for the morning.**

Use this concept as conversation material, not as a claim that a venue partnership already exists.
