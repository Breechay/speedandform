# FORM House
## A physical home for the practice · October 2026

## Thesis

**Luck is opportunity meeting preparation.**

FORM no longer has Hideout as its informal physical home. The practice still has recurring early mornings, coached athletes, shared long runs, Track Thursday, photography, studies, FORM software and a growing network — but no dependable place to gather before or after the work.

FORM House is the prepared answer.

It is not necessarily one building, one neighborhood or one ownership model. It is a physical layer for the FORM methodology that can live:
- inside an apartment building;
- inside a hotel;
- beside or within a café;
- inside an existing gym/performance facility;
- as an annex or residency;
- eventually as a standalone FORM location.

The concept should be ready before the right landlord, hotelier, developer, café owner, gym operator or capital partner appears.

## Core sentence

**FORM makes training an occasion. FORM House gives the occasion a home.**

## What the space does

At minimum:
- opens early enough for 5:30–6:30 AM running;
- gives runners a reliable meeting point;
- allows bags / small personal items to be left safely while people run;
- provides water and a place to reset after training;
- provides seating / lounge space for 10–30 minutes before or after;
- supports coffee / simple food either directly or through the host;
- has a bathroom or practical restroom access;
- makes recovery / mobility possible without pretending to be a medical clinic;
- can hold a small FORM board / session information;
- can support photographs, study moments and community continuity.

Useful additions depending on partner:
- showers / changing;
- cold plunge / sauna / compression / recovery equipment;
- strength / mobility corner;
- lockers;
- sports nutrition / breakfast;
- retail shelf;
- bike parking;
- route maps;
- screening / assessment area;
- small event / workshop capacity;
- projector / film / study nights;
- concierge-style access for hotel/apartment guests.

## What FORM brings

FORM is not asking only for rent-free space.

The practice can bring:
- recurring organized early-morning use;
- an established running/coaching identity;
- authored programming rather than generic “run club” activity;
- hosted Thursday and Saturday occasions;
- coaching / assessment services;
- FORM software and participant continuity;
- photography / editorial storytelling;
- selected public studies and performance education;
- relationships with runners, coaches, gyms and local operators;
- a reason for residents / guests / members to use a property before normal daytime traffic;
- a credible performance-running layer a host does not need to invent.

## Three partnership models

### 01 · Residency
FORM uses a defined lounge / café / lobby / amenity space during agreed early hours.

Best for:
- apartment / condominium;
- hotel;
- café;
- existing gym.

Host provides:
- access;
- bathroom / bag-hold agreement;
- seating / recovery basics;
- optional coffee / breakfast / amenity access.

FORM provides:
- hosted training mornings;
- programming;
- participant communication;
- agreed brand / editorial presence;
- clean closeout / operating protocol.

### 02 · FORM Annex
A host dedicates or co-builds a small physical zone specifically for the practice.

Potential components:
- lockers / bag wall;
- coffee / hydration;
- mobility / recovery;
- assessment table / movement area;
- FORM board / archive wall;
- small retail / nutrition;
- showers where feasible.

Commercial structures could include rent, rev share, amenity programming fee, resident benefit, sponsorship or mixed structure. Terms are partner-specific; do not pre-price publicly without a real site/operator.

### 03 · Standalone FORM House
Longer-horizon capital project: a purpose-built performance-running home where training, recovery, coaching, media, study and community meet.

This is not an immediate lease search. It is the full expression of the operating concept if the right capital, site and demand converge.

## Why a host might care

Apartment / residential:
- differentiated resident amenity;
- authentic morning programming;
- retention / community value;
- content and neighborhood identity.

Hotel:
- performance-travel amenity;
- morning running concierge / route support;
- recurring local energy;
- guest + local crossover;
- recovery / breakfast opportunity.

Café:
- dependable early-morning traffic;
- post-run dwell;
- association with a credible practice;
- recurring occasions rather than one-off events.

Gym / performance facility:
- running system layered onto strength/performance;
- complementary audience;
- route-based outdoor training;
- coaching / study collaboration.

Developer / capital partner:
- operating concept with software, programming, network and brand already in motion;
- can be embedded into existing real estate rather than requiring a new consumer brand from zero.

## What this is not

- not a generic run-club clubhouse;
- not a nightclub run concept;
- not a coworking space with treadmills;
- not a medical recovery clinic;
- not a social feed made physical;
- not dependent on a huge membership count before it has value.

The differentiator is **structured performance running + continuity + hospitality**.

## First operating prototype

A partner does not need to fund a permanent build first.

Pilot:
- 4–8 Saturday mornings or selected Thursdays;
- agreed early access window;
- bag hold;
- water / coffee;
- seating;
- optional mobility/recovery access;
- simple attendance / host closeout;
- photo / editorial permission handled explicitly;
- measure arrival, dwell, repeat attendance, host friction and participant value.

A successful residency is evidence for a larger annex.

## Space requirements · early filter

Must have:
- safe 5:30/6:00 AM access;
- practical bag policy;
- bathroom access;
- meeting visibility;
- route access without a long vehicle transfer;
- operator who understands recurring use.

Strong positives:
- showers;
- coffee / food;
- recovery amenity;
- outdoor terrace / lobby;
- proximity to Venetian Causeway, Miami Beach, waterfront, Brickell, Edgewater or other useful running corridors;
- parking / bike access.

Location is secondary to operational fit. FORM can be built in the right place, not only one neighborhood.

## Activation sequence

1. Build the shareable pitch page.
2. Keep a short list of candidate hosts / operators / developers as they emerge naturally.
3. Visit the place before proposing a structure.
4. Identify the host's actual problem / asset.
5. Propose the smallest useful residency.
6. Pilot.
7. Record evidence.
8. Expand only when usage and host economics support it.

## Evidence to collect from current practice

- number of recurring Thursday/Saturday occurrences;
- actual attendance, not inflated RSVP;
- repeat faces;
- pre/post-run dwell behavior;
- demand for bag drop / coffee / shower / recovery;
- route usefulness;
- photographs showing the practice;
- coaching / plan conversion where appropriate;
- partner-side traffic / amenity use if a pilot occurs.

Do not invent projections before a pilot.
