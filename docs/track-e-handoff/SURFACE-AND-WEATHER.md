# Surface and weather

Copied from live site law, 19 Aug 2026 (`rd48`). App weather is this package.

The app is an instrument. It was built to look complete while bare. That is still the law.

Brice is a colorful person. The technical system underneath is quiet on purpose. Personality does not enter as chrome, chapters, or a redesigned Today. It enters as **weather around the instrument**.

## The two rooms

**Site = understanding.** Plate 03 on `speedandform.com/` is where the world is allowed to show: bone paper, the dial as a sun, a cropped dragon as atmosphere.

**App = action.** Today stays one dominant read. The dragon lives in unused field — not over the work. Same animal, same orange and teal. Not a poster laid on top of the session.

If a pass makes Today look like an art book, a museum catalog, or a wallpaper, it is wrong. Steal the confidence. Do not steal the layout.

## The sun

The disc is the sun. It is Today. It names the day. It does not need a second sun, a sky gradient, or a weather widget beside it.

## The dragon

Not a character. Not a third object to compose against. Weather.

- Own image layer. Never a background.
- Saturated. No wash, blur, grain, tint, or blend on the file. Atmosphere is a **mask on the container**, not a treatment of the art. The pigment stays. The form dissolves toward the room.
- Cropped by the viewport. You never see the whole contour. It enters from outside and leaves again.
- The crop must read as **deliberate** before it is understood. Enough curve and scale to be a form entering the frame. A shard in the corner looks accidental.
- Bottom-right. Orange and teal must **consume that corner** — no cream pocket, or it reads as a stamp on the page.
- Mid-right of a phone stays almost empty. Fins do not climb the edge into the middle of the screen.
- Does not cover the disc. Does not cover FILE SESSION. Does not compete with the W1 tab.
- Arrives with the room. Not a flying entrance.
- Site crop: `min(86vw, 560px)`, right −14% / bottom −6%, image offset 2/2%, opacity 92%.
- Atmosphere mask: radial, origin low-right of the box (`88% 108%`). Corner holds. The edge facing the sun and the copy falls away. The drift animation is on the image, the mask stays put — the form moves through the veil, not with it.
- `weatherDrift`: 54s, ease-in-out, alternating, −8px / −10px and −0.6°, origin 80% 80%. Slow enough that you never catch it moving, only notice that it has. Anything faster is animation, and animation is a performance.

On the app: the same crop, unused lower field. It may shift slightly with the day. Slightly. A few percent, not a parade. Rest days can be quieter. Interval days do not need a performance. If quiet-by-day needs a system, ship one crop and stop.

## Clouds — parked

Not on the plate. Not on Today. Do not restore unless Brice asks.

Two directions were tried on the site (wash, then kumo). Wash was a dirt smudge (blur + multiply, too brown). Kumo was the right medium (cool ink, no blur) but the crop was wrong — first a snail tail, then a mass cut off on the right.

Do not blur. Do not `multiply`. Do not restore the snail tail. Do not attach clouds to weather data.

## Weather data

Do not. Sun, field, weather works because nothing labels it.

The moment atmosphere needs a forecast API or a plan tier to justify itself, it stops being a picture and becomes a feature. If real conditions ever influence it, beautiful — but it never has to earn its existence with a number.

## Amendments (Aug 19)

**A crop has to read as a form entering the frame.** The tightest pass on the tail left a shard in the corner that read as something the viewport accidentally clipped. Enough curve and scale to be read as deliberate before it is understood — never a terminus, never anatomy. Roughly a quarter arch around the sun, held to the lower right, mid-right stays empty.

**Weather dissolves the way weather dissolves.** The mask sits on the container, not on the file. Corner holds. The edge facing the room falls into paper. The image drifts through that veil.

**Weather moves the way weather moves.** 54s. Ease-in-out. Alternate. −8px / −10px and −0.6°. Origin 80% 80%.

## App lock (this work)

One crop. Unused field. Behind chrome if needed. Never over Today’s dominant action. The app stays complete while bare. Weather is the only addition that survives the 100m lock — and only if it reduces nothing, covers nothing, and can be ignored.

## Do not

- A dragon head, eyes, claws, or a full beast
- Recolor, desaturate, or drop opacity until it is a stain
- Blur or blend the dragon file to fake atmosphere — the mask is the veil
- Put a cloud on Today
- A weather widget, a sky gradient, or a second sun
- Treating the site plate as a mock of the iOS screen, or the iOS screen as a poster
- Reveal on compliance. Never a mascot, never a reward
