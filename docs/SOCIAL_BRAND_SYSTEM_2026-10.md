# Speed & Form social brand system

**Working authority · October 4, 2026**

This document governs Instagram, future YouTube, social post design, social photography, outbound email presentation and platform naming. It does not replace the current website doctrine. Read `docs/SPEED_AND_FORM_DOCTRINE.md` and `docs/BRAND.md` first.

## 1. Brand architecture

**Speed & Form is the public house.**

It is the name used for the website, business, coaching, public authority, email domain, YouTube channel and partner-facing work.

**FORM is the practice and product layer.**

It names the running practice, community expressions, app, studies and related objects such as FORM Thursday, FORM Morning and FORM House.

**Forge remains the strength product.**

The app may keep the FORM identity, green field and lime product signal. Social and partner-facing surfaces should not inherit the app icon merely because the app uses it.

### Marks

- **SF emblem**: primary public and social mark. Use for Instagram avatar, YouTube avatar, social sign-offs, email header and partner-facing documents.
- **FORM. app mark**: app icon, native product and explicit app demonstrations only.
- **FORM wordmark / italic FORM**: may appear as an editorial series label inside a post, study or film. It is not the primary social avatar.

## 3. Social visual field

The social brand should feel compatible with serious hospitality, wellness and sport-led properties without pretending to be a hotel or luxury-development company.

The governing idea is:

> **Athletic practice, photographed with hospitality-level care.**

Use the current Speed & Form commercial field:

- Black `#0d0f0e`
- Ink `#161916`
- Bone `#e8e3d9`
- Paper `#f1ede4`

Green and lime remain product signals for the FORM app, Labs and explicit app/technical posts. They do not become the default Instagram field.

Typography:

- Inter Tight for display and body.
- JetBrains Mono or the existing monospace for dates, distances, locations, paces and study labels.
- No decorative script, faux-luxury serif or generic motivational typography.

## 4. Photography direction

The feed should not look like a run-club flyer archive. It should show a complete world around serious training.

### Core sequence

**Arrival → preparation → work → human moment → return**

Capture:

- athletes arriving before dawn;
- bags, shoes, water, hands, watches and preparation;
- real movement with complete bodies and readable mechanics;
- coaching exchanges and quiet concentration;
- the return: towel, drink, food, seat, conversation and recovery;
- Miami as environment: waterfront, track, bridge, street, shade and early light;
- portraits that feel observed rather than cast.

Real fit, attractive and well-presented athletes may naturally establish the aspirational world. Never write appearance as a participation requirement, and do not turn the practice into a casting exercise.

### Image treatment

- Real athletes and real sessions only.
- Preserve skin tone and identity. No generated people or identity-changing retouching.
- First-fold / hero imagery may be black and white where the website system calls for it.
- Social photography can use restrained natural color: warm dawn, neutral skin, deep black, bone, concrete, water and vegetation.
- Avoid orange/teal grading, crushed-detail pseudo-luxury, HDR, fake film borders and heavy presets.
- Prefer one strong frame to a collage.
- Crop for complete action and human context, not only shoes and watches.

### Working mix

- 45% training / movement
- 20% people / portraits / coaching
- 15% return / hospitality / food / space
- 10% study record / evidence
- 10% invitations / products / partner concepts

The mix is directional, not a posting quota.

## 5. Post system

Use three primary post forms. Do not force a checkerboard grid.

### A. The photograph

A single full-frame 4:5 photograph. Minimal or no type on the image.

Optional furniture:

- small SF emblem;
- one mono line with date, place or session;
- no more than one short title.

### B. The record

For milestones, studies and coaching reads.

Cover:

- black or bone field;
- one clear object, number or sentence;
- optional small real photograph;
- study/date label in mono.

Examples:

- `6 CONTINUOUS MILES.`
- `NO RESETS.`
- `A TRAINING MILESTONE. NOT A PREDICTION.`
- `WHAT HELD AFTER 60 MINUTES?`

Inside slides may show progression, session structure, one coaching decision and the next question. Do not turn a carousel into a mini research paper.

### C. The invitation

For Thursday, a confirmed occurrence, coaching or a real partner morning.

Use:

- one date;
- one place;
- one action;
- a photograph whenever possible;
- explicit status when proposed rather than confirmed.

No generic event poster language, fake urgency or weekly promise that has not been earned.

## 6. Reel and video direction

Reels should feel like short observational films, not trend participation.

- 8–25 seconds for social practice films.
- Natural sound first: footsteps, breath, watch beeps, water, plates, ambient city.
- Music only when it supports the scene and rights are clean.
- Open on action or atmosphere, not a logo animation.
- Use one quiet title and one restrained end card.
- No speed ramps, fake camera shutters, aggressive transitions or template packs.

Working sequence:

1. place / arrival;
2. preparation;
3. effort;
4. human exchange;
5. return.

### YouTube thumbnails

- one strong image;
- three to six words;
- small series label in mono;
- SF emblem in one consistent corner;
- no red circles, arrows, exaggerated reaction faces or crowded copy.

Examples:

- `WHY THE PACE FADED`
- `THE HOLD IS THE TEST`
- `A MORNING WORTH RETURNING TO`
- `WHAT CHANGES NEXT?`

## 7. Caption voice

Short, factual and close to the work.

Preferred structure:

1. what happened;
2. what it means or what remains open;
3. one next step when relevant.

Examples:

> Six continuous miles. No resets.  
> A training milestone, not a race prediction.

> Thursday. Flamingo Park.  
> Meet at 6. Run at 6:15. The workout posts Wednesday.

> Come for the miles. Stay for the morning.  
> A proposed partner concept. No property or date is confirmed.

Avoid:

- inspirational filler;
- generic wellness language;
- excessive hashtags;
- corporate “we believe” copy;
- explaining every feature in one caption;
- calling ordinary practice “elite” or “luxury.”

## 10. What this system is borrowing from the benchmark

The benchmark is not beige wellness styling. It is discipline:

- one clear idea per frame;
- excellent photography;
- substantial negative space;
- materials, service and place made tangible;
- confidence without excessive explanation;
- sport, wellness and social life presented as one considered environment.

Speed & Form should retain its own authority: real training, real coaching judgment, real athletes and Miami. It should not imitate a property developer's render language or manufacture amenities it does not own.
