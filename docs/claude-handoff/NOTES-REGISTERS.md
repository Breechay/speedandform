# Notes — two registers, one grammar

Amends the approved Notes design brief. It does not replace it. Line / Reason / Close stands. The bronze stands. The inset modal stands. One Line across card, modal and overlay stands.

## What changed

Notes are not a content library. They are the distribution.

Athletes share these because they are proud of the treatment. That is a stranger seeing the work from someone they already trust, which is the only channel that has ever worked here. A note aimed at strangers is content. A note about a named athlete, given to that athlete, is a gift they hand to their own network. Same object, different economics.

Everything below follows from that.

## The two registers

**Observation** — a frame of a named athlete and what Brice saw in it. Cannot be generic, because it is about a real person in a real moment. Carries the photography. This is the one that gets shared, and the one a browsing athlete reads as proof.

**Reminder** — the plain ones. Short, no person, no session. Form cues, what to do with a thing, what he repeats every week. Notes 011–014 are Reminders. These travel to strangers and to search later.

Both are Notes. Both use Line / Reason / Close. They are siblings, not sections.

## The register mark is the name

No labels. No "OBSERVATION" eyebrow, no filter chips, no taxonomy on the surface — that announces discernment instead of demonstrating it.

**An Observation carries an athlete's name. A Reminder does not.** That is the entire visual difference, plus the bronze rule already in the brief.

One number sequence across both. NOTE 015 may be an Observation and NOTE 016 a Reminder. The library is one library.

## Observation grammar

- **Frame** — the photograph. The athlete, mid-work, in the treatment Brice gives their images.
- **Name** — mono, small, bronze rule to its left. The register mark.
- **Line** — what he saw. Same Line slot, same string across all three surfaces. Still a discernment a runner can repeat, not a caption.
- **Reason** — two or three short paragraphs about that frame. What he saw. What the mechanism is, if he will own it.
- **Close** — same slot, same bronze, same weight, different job by register:
  - **Reminder:** the decision it changes. Training is the currency.
  - **Observation:** the sensation to go find, and what produces it. The body is the currency. A decision is the wrong close here — it diagnoses and leaves the runner with nothing to do.

The trail leg in NOTE 001 is the example: it cannot be cued. Tell someone to extend it and they reach, overstride, brake. So an Observation Close hands over the inputs that produce the thing, then names the sensation so they know when they have found it.

The Line must survive the athlete being removed. If it only makes sense as a caption for that photo, it is a caption, not a Note.

## Named, not anonymous

Named makes it evidence and makes it shareable. Simon sees Simon. Pride requires a name, and pride is the mechanism.

Athletes are signed. No per-note release is required. Still do not invent a name or a frame.

## Cut: maintenance workouts

A workout is a prescription, not a Note. Publishing uncalibrated prescriptions gives away the thing being sold, and it contradicts what 02 argues — that the judgment is Brice's and that sequence carries most of the effect. A stranger running the maintenance circuit out of order is the exact failure he described being annoyed by.

Maintenance work belongs in FORM, or in an athlete's own record. Not in the public library.

## Cut: 100 as a target

The right number is however many exist without one being filler. The working estimate is already ~50. Ship the door with 14 behind it. A library that grows because athletes keep handing over frames is a consequence, not a plan.

## Plate 03 — the door, not the gallery

Plate 03 is back, with a different job. It died the first time for restating 01 and 02. It returns as **evidence**, which neither 01 nor 02 provides.

- 01 is the offer. 02 is the mind. 03 is the proof that the mind has been applied to real people.
- **One note. Not a grid.** A grid turns the instrument back into a website, which is how 03 died.
- **The material changes.** 01 is a colour film, 02 is a grey film, 03 is a still photograph. The instrument stops moving when it shows you evidence. That is the whole reason 03 is allowed to exist as a third room.
- Same handoff law: the still fades in over film B, film B never moves. One property.
- Contents: the Line, the athlete's name with its bronze rule, and one door to `/notes`. Nothing else.
- Begin persists and does not move.

**01 + first thesis + Begin is still complete.** 02 and 03 are both depth. Unchanged and non-negotiable.

**The test that kills 03 again:** if the Line on it could have been said on 02, it is a restatement and the plate goes. 03 earns its place only by naming a person.
