# Return 02 — the 02 text axis

Four arguments. Ceiling met, not exceeded. Every claim and every proof is drawn from Brice's ASK-BRICE answers; nothing invented.

## The four

**01 — I develop runners.** *(locked, unchanged)*
Training is an argument made over time. I assess where you are, prescribe the work, observe what it establishes, and decide what comes next.

**02 — I watch for ease.**
Foot landing, arm swing, the rhythm between them. Never one thing at a time — what I am reading is whether the whole stride agrees with itself. Speed is what shows up when it does.

**03 — You are an accumulation.**
No session stands by itself. Each one is written on top of the last, and that order carries most of the effect. Swap one and the running still happened — the thread I was reading did not.

**04 — I want the quiet weeks.**
Fresh, fed, slept, moving well. That is the week worth having, not the heroic one. Stack eight of them and the same paces cost less — more ground, less negotiation.

Three first person, one turn to second person on 03. That turn is the only place the page addresses the reader before Begin, which is why it sits third and not first.

Form → speed lives inside 02 as a consequence, not as a slogan. It is never its own plate and never a tagline.

Two answers were deliberately not made into arguments. "It's not about wanting it" (fit) belongs in the intake, not on a page someone is deciding on. Lifting-belongs-always is an offer fact, not a practice argument.

One phrase is now dead: *"I answer when a decision is required."* Not his — he always answers. It reads as rationing. Do not reuse it or anything shaped like it.

The brief's rule that FORM-the-house and form-in-a-stride are different words is overruled by Brice directly: "I dont think there is one." Same word, one meaning. The page should never explain the distinction.
