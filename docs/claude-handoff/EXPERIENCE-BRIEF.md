## Motion that sells without selling

High-end movie sites fail when every object is interactive. They succeed when **one gesture drives several related things**, then rest.

Already true here:

- Scroll drives film A out, film B in, 01 copy leaving, 02 copy arriving
- Begin does not move
- Both films are already playing (no hitch)

Elevate from that, not beside it:

- 01→02: keep the overlap dissolve. If you add slats, they are the *type* arriving, after the films have mostly crossed.
- Begin: still. Never re-enter.
- 02 arguments: the outgoing claim leaves the way 01 leaves (up and gone). The next claim arrives on the same curve, 40–80ms later. No carousel dots if the ticks can include a small 1 of 3 in mono under the thesis — or nothing. Empty is correct if the swipe is obvious from the text moving.
