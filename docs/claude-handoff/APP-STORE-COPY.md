**Description**

Open it and the day is decided. The work, the paces, and the part of the race it is building.

Half Marathon, Marathon, and HYROX running.

**Sessions with a job**
A race asks for four things: a start you don't spend, a rhythm you settle into, a middle you hold, and a finish you still own. Pressure to Pace. Clean Rhythm. Long aerobic support. Race rehearsal. Each session is written against one of them.

**Paces you can act on**
Every piece of work carries its target. 30 min steady at 6:49–7:03. 3 × 8 min at 5:43. 2 min float between. Nothing to decode at the track.

**A week that fits the life you have**
Choose how many days you run and which day is long. The week is written from your race date and rewritten as it gets closer.

**Training that is filed, not logged**
File a session and it becomes evidence. What you actually did decides what comes next.

Running does not have to be boring. It has to be built.

## What's New — version 38

A new surface, and a new engine underneath it.

FORM now opens on the dial. Today at the centre, your week around it, the paces you need, and nothing else asking for attention. Session details are one tap away.

Underneath, the week is written differently. Half Marathon training is now built against the parts of a race — the start, the settle, the hold, the finish — instead of variations on the same interval. Pressure to Pace, Clean Rhythm, Far Side, Converging Long, The Move, Last Mile Honest, Gear Check. A demanding long run is counted against the week's hard work, so a week never quietly becomes three hard days.

Your race countdown and your program week are now two separate clocks, both truthful.

Fixed: a caching fault that could show one session before you tapped a day and a different one after. The day you tap is the day you were given.

Now on iPad.
