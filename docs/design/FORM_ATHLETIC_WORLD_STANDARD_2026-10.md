# FORM athletic world · product and visual standard
**Owner-approved direction · October 19, 2026**  
**Scope:** Speed & Form Running, side door, Dossier, The group, Photos and film, athlete profiles, website homepage, Runs/Collective, Labs previews, media-led marketing, and future curated notes. Strength/Forge may reuse this world where relevant without changing its separate training-room identity.

## The rule

**FORM is not only a planner or an event calendar. It is an athletic world.** Athletes come back for their training, but also for the people, photography, films, useful ideas and real work happening around them.

**No dead sections.** If there is no published upcoming run, the Group/Collective feature should still lead somewhere worth opening. The priority shifts from **upcoming event → genuinely published recent work → approved evergreen photograph, film or useful note**. An old run is never presented as upcoming; the absence of a new date remains clear wherever the athlete seeks logistics. An offline/error state is not an event-free state.

**People are the subject. FORM is the platform. The founder is not the narrator.** The athletic world is about the athletes and the practice, not repeat signatures, gratuitous photo credits or explanations of who approves tags. Keep genuinely necessary source and contributor attribution, real personal identities, consent and privacy disclosures.

**Photography and film are primary design material, not filler.** Use the existing, rights-cleared FORM library, published group albums and real sessions. Compose with large images, crop-aware focal points, strong type, tonal grading, documentary energy, strips, depth, tactile transitions and thoughtful visual pacing. Do not flatten a rich screen into identical ruled rows or identical tiles. Do not generate fictional athlete photographs or imply that a photo proves attendance.

**Small useful ideas should be complete in the app.** A field note, coaching cue, mindset observation or study snapshot can be read in a few seconds without leaving for an article. Offer an optional source link only where one exists and adds value. Keep it specific to the coaching practice, dated or sourced as appropriate, never abstract motivational filler. Third-party examples (including Steve Magness posts) are inspiration for *format and usefulness*, not a license to republish unlicensed content.

**Archive beats a disappearing feed.** Albums, films, athlete pages, studies and notes remain findable by meaningful subject, run, date or athlete. No endless algorithmic feed, likes, follows, leaderboard, comments, fake activity counts, daily-post obligation or new social graph. Curation is editorial, not engagement bait.

## Relevance requirement · no padded sections (October 19 refinement)

**No dead sections does not mean never hide a module. No dead sections. No padded sections.** A surface must give the athlete a meaningful next route or a truthful current state, but each optional tile, film rail or note may disappear if nothing specific and eligible deserves the space. Three honest elements are better than nine weak substitutes. A quiet week is a valid editorial condition, not a deficit to disguise.

Lead selection must pass the **relevance test** in addition to publication/consent/rights checks. The item should relate to at least one of: this real group's people/place/work; an actual upcoming event; a verified recently published session or album; the relevant athlete's **publicly shareable** phase or study; a deliberately resurfaced archive item with a stated reason; or an existing FORM Field Note with a concrete connection to current practice. Do not fill Standing with random evergreen motivation.

Prefer **live → recent → contextually standing → meaningfully resurfaced archive**, but treat 48-hour and 7-day cutoffs as examples, **not universal gates**. A published run on the calendar is still a real upcoming run when more than 48 hours away; a six-week-old film can be more relevant than an unrelated note published today. State actual event date and original capture/publish dates. Surface a truthful `No group run scheduled` on the schedule path when verified; never invent a `next` date. Unavailable network reads are not proof of no schedule.

Use **eligible source reference → deliberate selection → designed rendering**. There may be a tiny reusable content-card/feature projection across app and site, but it must only point to original approved records, not become another content platform, reposting pipeline or independent permission authority. Avoid the term `Plate` as a binding new storage schema: FORM already uses it in other visual/instrument contexts. A visual card is a presentation of an eligible event, album, film, public note, study or photo.

Don't create a scheduled publishing obligation (weekly numbered Issues), rest-day media injections, an iOS widget, personal performance receipts, athlete quote forms, a place atlas, new public permalink system or in-café displays as part of v1. These are separate design proposals requiring evidence and approval. Athlete stories or personal metrics need explicit, contextual consent and should not be turned into group accountability mechanics by default.

## Signature photographic architecture

Use the approved [Bend Photographic Aperture](FORM_BEND_PHOTOGRAPHIC_APERTURE_2026-10.md) when an actual subject deserves theatrical treatment. Measured bend lanes may frame a real FORM photograph; the SF emblem may anchor the scene; large editorial type can counterbalance the image. This is a flexible design grammar, not a template pasted into every module, a command to put the bend behind the functional Week or permission to publish a mock's sample people. Real photography and clear training facts remain primary.

## Composition and interaction

- Preserve the **approved cream ink-bend native entrance** and the dark Week, not a photographic replacement splash. Place photographic immersion within the authenticated side door, Group and media rooms, and appropriately within approved web landing compositions.
- The **Week remains the training instrument**. It must not become a feed. The athlete name opens the left-hand cream side door; a narrow strip of Week remains visible. Secondary destinations open as their own pages. Respect the gesture conflict boundary, Reduce Motion, Dynamic Type and 44-pt tap targets.
- The side door stays tactile and informative: the race/countdown card, focus, Plan, Messages, study, The group, athlete faces, Photos and film, own page and Account. The Group tile can feature an upcoming run or the latest eligible media/evergreen item. Never show an RSVP button without a real confirmed public occurrence.
- **The group** is an editorial destination. One large current lead, a portrait-led athlete gallery, a film, past-run albums and optional useful note; use variation in image scale and layout. No generic ruled menu taking the place of the content. Keep utility rows where they genuinely outperform cards.
- **Photos and film** is a persistent library destination; latest activity does not determine its existence. Full-screen images preserve meaningful names only from approved tags and visible profiles. `That's me` sends a real request; neutral progress copy, actual confirmation gates.
- **Public website homepage** keeps its existing commercially useful entrance and cinematic photography. The current hero, athlete gallery, film and photo strips should become better connected to curated work, not duplicated as a new homepage feed. Let the website teach deeper material while the app's note stands alone.
- **Race destination photography** can set context behind an athlete's actual assigned race countdown, using the approved black-and-white city imagery and a crop/grade tailored to each photograph. An unavailable asset falls back to the established dark field. The countdown, event and training block remain source-backed, never a mock value.
- **No uniform monochrome mandate:** use natural and black-and-white images intentionally. Protect dark skin, facial detail, highlights, text contrast, loading performance and motion accessibility. Keep each image's permitted crop and actual rights.

## Design inspiration is not published FORM evidence

The October 19 group/homepage mockups demonstrate editorial scale, bold imagery, typography, variation, film and inhabitation. Their sample brand labels (`STRIDE`, `PACE`), runners, city names (`London`, `Portland`), dates, distances, attendance, personal scores, quotes, media playback controls and copy are **not** approved FORM facts or identities. Do not copy them into a production app/site. Use only verified FORM albums, real group runs, approved photographs, published athletes and genuine note/study content. Do not add a permanent playback bar, new bottom navigation, home hero overhaul or extra content rails simply because an illustrative mock happens to show them.

## Guardrails

1. Reuse existing authority. Media catalogue: `/media/library/library.json`. People and tags: `docs/collective/PEOPLE-AND-TAGS-CONTRACT-20261013.md`. Public sessions/films: `docs/collective/SESSIONS-AND-FILM-CONTRACT-20261014.md` and `docs/collective/NATIVE-SUPABASE-FILM-20261014.md`. Occurrences and RSVP: the existing published Collective public run projections. Studies: the already approved public Labs pages. **Do not add a new database, posting system or parallel identity.**
2. Only published, permitted, non-withdrawn content is eligible. A hidden profile stays hidden in directory, captions, confirmed tags, cover selectors and films, and pulled media cannot linger as a stale featured item. Ask for separate approval before repurposing private athlete data or unpublished studies.
3. Never invent an event, date, participants, quote, run location, message preview, program volume, album, note or photo count to fill a layout. No misleading `new`, `now` or `latest` labels for old items. If no approved media can be shown, use an honest, useful destination, never a false populated card.
4. Editorial sources may be pinned/curated manually. Do not require infinite scrolling, reactions, frequent uploads or auto-generated platitudes. Value is in reuse and discoverability of existing work.
5. Keep contract changes and production separate: policy/docs first, component integration later, device captures and privacy checks before shipping. **Commit freely, deploy deliberately.**

## Acceptance on every release

- **With a future published run:** featured event shows its real date/time/place and valid `I'm in`; albums, film and athletes remain accessible.
- **Without a future run:** no RSVP and no fabricated date; an authorized film/album/photo/note is visible as lead and permanent media doors remain useful.
- **With zero recent media:** approved evergreen content or browseable archive, not a blank broken section; dated content isn't relabeled fresh.
- **With withdrawn/hid content:** the person and media stop appearing in all public app/web projections, including cover and offline paths, per existing publication contracts.
- **On older/smaller phones and slow/offline networks:** content remains navigable, meaningful text isn't truncated, loading and read failures are distinguished, and fallback never leaks private data.
- **Visual review:** judge actual photos, portrait crop, skin/tonal detail, film playback, hierarchy, motion, and app/site coherence. Premium means compelling, not plain.
