## Immediate coaching-system lesson

Anthony is newer to the FORM weekly hierarchy.

Teach this explicitly:

- Tuesday / the principal race-pace session owns the week's first question.
- Monday protects it.
- Thursday is support and is **earned by Tuesday's absorption**.
- Saturday is not automatically a third hard day.
- Missed or reduced support is not made up.

The rule is about priority, not about Tuesday as a sacred weekday. If the main session moves, the protection moves with it.

## Current week

- clean execution without rescue surges;
- credible reserve;
- ordinary recovery;
- repeatable weeks.

Completing a harder support workout does not outrank the primary session.
