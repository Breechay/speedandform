# Jacob · Study 005 intake · coach questions

Use this before freezing FORM or FORGE.

## 1. Knee / hip + PT
1. Where exactly do the knee and hip problems show up — right/left, front/side/back?
2. What do you feel during a run, immediately after, and the next morning?
3. What usually brings it on: distance, hills, speed, stairs, sitting, strength work, something else?
4. Any swelling, instability, locking/catching, numbness, or change in gait?
5. What has your PT/physio or doctor told you so far, if anything? Any explicit restrictions?
6. Send the actual PT/physio exercises you were given, including sets/reps/frequency, and tell me which ones you actually do consistently.
7. When was the last reassessment and what would make you go back sooner?

## 2. Running / ultra history
8. Give me your 3–5 most representative ultras: distance, date, terrain, finish time, and how the last third felt.
9. What is the longest event you have completed, and what is the longest training week you have handled well?
10. What did a normal healthy training week look like when you felt strongest: days running, weekly distance, long run, workouts?
11. Over the last 8–12 weeks, roughly how much have you actually run each week and what has the long run been?
12. What changed before the current knee/hip issues became noticeable?

## 3. Palermo
13. Confirm the exact Palermo Marathon date, travel dates, and whether you still want to start regardless of current symptoms.
14. When you say "finish, not compete," what does that mean practically: continuous running, run/walk, no time goal, cutoff only?
15. What would make you decide not to start, shorten the day, or seek another clinical review before the race?

## 4. Cross-training
16. How many days per week are you currently doing elliptical, spin and stair stepper, and for how long?
17. Which of those are symptom-free? Which ever irritate the knee/hip?
18. Send the exact version of your favorite elliptical, spin and stair workouts — levels/resistance, duration, and HR/RPE if you know it.

## 5. Strength / equipment
19. What strength training have you done consistently in the past, if any?
20. What equipment do you have access to right now: gym, machines, dumbbells, barbells, kettlebells, bands, bench, sled, etc.?
21. Which leg-strength movements feel good, and which currently bother the knee or hip?
22. Can you comfortably squat, split squat/lunge, hinge/RDL, step up, calf raise, single-leg balance and carry weight?

## 6. Recovery / fueling / logistics
23. Typical sleep on workdays and weekends?
24. How do you fuel and hydrate long runs now, especially beyond 2 hours?
25. Any recent bodyweight change that matters to you or affects how you feel running?
26. How many days per week can you realistically train, and how much time can you give strength on those days?
27. Which days are fixed by work/family/travel and which are flexible?

## 7. The two-year horizon
28. For the 100-miler, what attracts you: a specific race, mountains/scenery/adventure, finishing, competing, or seeing how good you can get?
29. Trail or road? Technical terrain or runnable? Heat, altitude, climbing?
30. Is there a 50K/50-mile/100K progression you already want on the way, or should we design that ladder from scratch?

## Evidence rule

Athlete report, device estimates, PT/physio instructions, race results and coach interpretation stay separate. Do not diagnose from the training data.
