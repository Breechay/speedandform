## The 2027 development runway, gated rather than dated

**01 · Durable easy running.** Several consecutive well-tolerated weeks with no concerning impact response, a repeatable easy longer outing and strength coexisting with the running. External medical guidance for post-fracture impact progression takes precedence.

**02 · Low-dose mechanics and economy.** When readiness is stable, coach may introduce short relaxed strides and controlled technique exposures. These are not timed mile trials or automatic threshold sessions. Motion is judged by fluidity, breathing, coordination and 24–48 h recovery; do not chase longer steps, lower GCT or a pace display.

**03 · Controlled aerobic quality.** One purposeful quality session in a week, with easy running and the longer aerobic outing supporting it. Build threshold and 10K-specific duration without requiring a simultaneous VO₂ interval day.

**04 · Speed and ceiling.** Progress to thoughtfully separated VO₂ and mile-speed exposures, using the established Thursday track repertoire as an exercise library rather than lifting historical workloads wholesale. Preserve enough easy work and strength for adaptation.

**05 · Race specificity and selection.** Choose a mile or 10K endpoint only when training has built repeated support for it. Test sub-5 mile (~74.8 seconds per 400 m) and 6:00/mi 10K (~37:17) through appropriate progressive evidence. There is no need to repeat a marathon to validate durability.

## Weekly decision grammar

**What happened?** Running time and terrain, harder exposures, lower-body strength, Garmin source, aerobic measures.

**What did it cost?** Athlete breathing/leg/RPE, tibia symptoms during and after, HR relative to comparable conditions, next-day reserve.

**What changed?** Actual movement and consistency at matched conditions, not a one-off faster pace or rising watch VO₂.

**What is the next smallest useful test?** Hold, reduce, or modestly advance **one** training variable. Recovery is a valid result. No catch-up work.
