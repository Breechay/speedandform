### The same pace, carried farther
Broken work → 5 → 6 → 8 → late running → 12 → race.

Recovery disappears gradually. Late race-pace work asks whether the pace remains available after easy miles.

### Your race strategy starts in Week 1
Start. Settle. Hold. Finish.

Practise entering the band without chasing, staying there when you feel good, and finding it for short sections without looking at pace. Use the sessions to establish the opening and finish you can trust on race day.
