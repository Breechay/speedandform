# speedandform.com · house pass · 23 September 2026

This is the operating record for the September house cleanup. The goal is not visual sameness. The goal is a coherent house that tells the truth while each room keeps its own character.

## Decisions now locked

- **House model:** one house, many rooms.
- **Public weekly anchor:** Thursday speedwork. Its logistics are owned by `/js/community-schedule.js`.
- **Long runs:** occasional monthly pop-ups, announced when confirmed.
- **Training plans may still use fixed weekdays.** Those are authored program architecture, not the public schedule.

- **Edition 01 merch:** cap, 30 pieces. S+F seal on the front; individual number `01 / 30` through `30 / 30` inside or at the rear.

## Cache decision

Do **not** globally switch `/js/*` to year-long immutable caching yet. The shared community schedule lives in JavaScript and must stay easy to update. Improve caching only after every mutable public asset has a reliable versioned URL strategy.

## Film rule

For Thursday films: one clear sentence from Brice, real work, one useful cue, recovery/human texture, and a simple route back to `/thursday`. Get written permission from people who are identifiable in the final cut.
