# Speed & Form — Ecosystem Operating Plan
## October 2026 · v1 · 2026-10-02

This is the operating plan for turning Speed & Form into one coherent practice across the public website, shared mornings, paid coaching, plans, studies, photography/archive and FORM.

It is intentionally a one-month development plan, not a one-week feature sprint. The purpose is to make the practice easier to discover, join, understand, hire and continue inside FORM without turning the website or app into generic fitness software.

## North star

**Coaching, living studies, shared mornings, photography and a beautiful archive are the center.**

Public loop:

**Show up → experience the practice → see the work → choose more structure if wanted → train through a plan or Run Development → preserve the history in FORM.**

The website is the public discovery and explanation layer. FORM is the living execution and continuity layer. They meet at shared practice, selected proof and published archive material.

## Operating rules

1. Build from real weekly practice, not competitor feature lists.
2. One primary action per public surface. Coaching bridges are quiet and contextual.
3. Free tools remain genuinely useful; they do not become disguised sales funnels.
4. No scarcity copy or transformation claims.
5. Dynamic numbers such as current roster size should not become permanent copy unless there is a maintained source.
6. Saturday long runs are occurrences, not a promised monthly cadence unless Brice explicitly adopts that cadence.
7. FORM owns living occurrences, RSVP/attendance, athlete execution and private history. The website owns public discovery and evergreen explanation.
8. Selected FORM photographs/studies can become public proof; the website should not duplicate the full archive.
9. Daily Brief reads and prioritizes canonical sources; it does not become a second source of truth.
10. Stop redesigning once a surface is coherent enough to use. Real participation, inquiries, coaching and publishing provide the next evidence.

Belonging:

02 · Rest fully when the session calls for recovery.
03 · No pace is required to arrive.
04 · Leave when you need to.

Community bridge:
“Want the rest of the week built around you?”

- Thursday/Saturday: FORM owns occurrence, host, RSVP, attendance, Board and archive; website owns discovery/public live details.

- Plans: FORM owns assignment/ownership/execution when entitled; website owns discovery/preview/purchase.
- Studies: private evidence/continuity in coaching systems; curated public study on web.

1. Week — individualized daily home; Thursday only when relevant.
2. Shared mornings / Collective — Thursday + Saturday as same hosted-occurrence model; RSVP separate from attendance; minimal Board; past mornings accessible.
3. Archive · Roll → Nine — private Roll; Keep/Maybe/Set aside; select/reorder; cover/crop; Cream/Dark; exact preview; publish/recovery; Instagram + website export.
4. Dossier + Development — goal poster; phase/focus; key sessions; block reviews; coach notes; visible development arc.
5. Coach basics — current athletes; quick actions; messages; plan assignment/adjustment; simple notes.

Tier 2:
- named-session Vault;
- published-album public preview;
- plan ownership for paid self-directed plans when entitlement is ready;
- shared-morning notification opt-in.

Wait:
feeds/follows/leaderboards; member-created runs; broad member DMs; public athlete dossiers/goals; generic social layer; automatic coaching interpretation presented as coach judgment; competitor-parity features.

Feature filter:
1. Will Brice open it most weeks to coach or document?
2. Does it improve a real morning, athlete relationship or photograph?
3. Does it make history more useful or beautiful?
4. Would the practice still want it if no competing app had it?

current state; NOW/NEXT/LATER; dependencies; owner; completion evidence; deliberate deferrals; next review date.

Then stop ecosystem redesign for a period. Run Thursday. Host Saturday when useful. Coach. Photograph. Publish. Sell. Watch where people enter and what they choose.
