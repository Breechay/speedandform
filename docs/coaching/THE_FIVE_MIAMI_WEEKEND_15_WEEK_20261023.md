## Shared calendar, every week

- **Monday evening · iRUN:** currently assigned 40 min easy + 4 × 15 sec relaxed strides, with the exception of 30 min easy on the final two Mondays. This is the single **currently published** Monday prescription. Once the real iRUN workout is available, the coach reads its dose, reauthors that Monday’s FORM session **before the workout**, and reviews Thursday and surrounding Forge lower-body work. **Do not execute a hard iRUN workout and also do the easy fallback.** Historical October 19 iRUN was 8 × 800m Garmin-derived, not a prediction of future Mondays.
- **Wednesday:** 35 min easy; final two weeks 25 min easy. Easy means 8:00/mi or slower for the mile-based athletes, 4:58/km or slower for Tinius. There is **no 5:51/mi easy pace** prescribed.
- **Thursday · FORM Track:** quality anchor alternating 5K-speed development and controlled threshold. Common layout/host, not guaranteed identical physiology. Threshold is controlled RPE 6–7 with short phrases possible, **not** an invented individual pace. Warm-up 20 min and cool-down 15 min, except the final taper session.
- **Saturday:** genuinely easy aerobic durability, individualized; Kevin’s longer marathon-preparation outings carry fueling practice and a separate readiness check. Friday upper and Sunday moderate legs in Brice’s Forge design, Tinius’s Thursday-lower Forge conflict, and Kevin’s lower-body recovery plan require paired coach review whenever Monday/Thursday/Saturday change.

## Future athletes: same season, not a new season by default

Newly invited athletes join the **existing October 19–January 31 absolute calendar**, starting from the calendar-appropriate current Week, never automatically at Week 1 or with fabricated past completions. For an invited athlete, the onboarding transaction must create/verify one canonical athlete ID, correct private coaching membership for both coach identities, claimed invitation only after email/Apple security verification, correct athlete portrait, then copy the **remaining published sessions** from this versioned shared template while preserving individual pace units, RPE, injury limits and long-run target. No invented sign-in, no automatic blanket 26.2-mile goal. A group membership or community sign-in alone does not authorize a coached Week.

**Automation status:** A reusable manifest and instructions exist, but dynamic onboarding/automatic weekly iRUN updates are **not** deployed just by this document. Require new athlete assignment and signed-in Week view verification. Coaches may reauthor any later session with a new version without rewriting previous completions.
