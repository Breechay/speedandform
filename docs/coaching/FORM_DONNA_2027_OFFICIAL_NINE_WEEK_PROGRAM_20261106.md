## Non-negotiable architecture

- **Easy base is its own workout:** build consistent 45–75-minute comfortable aerobic runs; some should be longer than a 40-minute quality session including its warm-up/cooldown. Preserve at least two genuine easy-run days between major stressors where possible. Do not force volume after the half or a PT setback.
- **Long-run entry:** first establish an absorbable approximately **two-hour comfortable long run** before adding consequential MP segments. Use fueling practice on every sufficiently long run. Never infer that successful half pacing establishes long-run durability.
- **Marathon-specific work contains ≥30 cumulative actual MP minutes**, separate from floats, HMP and easy running. At first, repeated one-mile MP/one-mile easy **continuous alternations** can accumulate 30–60 MP minutes; these are not stopped mile repeats.
- **Build pace ownership before density:** first 60–70 minutes of continuous MP in a successful, recovered session; later consider 30-minute easy / 30-minute MP alternations to 2h30 only if the athlete already absorbs the simpler version. Recoveries progress from easy to individualized floats only after repeatable work.
- **Descending ladder:** one `4→3→2 km` descent (9 km specific + two 1 km easy transitions) or advanced `(4→3→2 km)×2` (18 km work + five 1 km easy transitions). The 4 km legs begin at MP; 3 km legs stay near MP or transition modestly; 2 km legs approach evidenced HMP if earned. The double is **not** compulsory. Avoid changing three load dimensions together.
- **High-cost alternatives, not a checklist:** `3×5 km` MP in a long run; the 2h30 Thirty/Thirty; the double descending ladder; and the 90–100-minute continuous MP test. Do **not** schedule all four. `3×10 km` is elite-only and excluded from this first standard program.
- **Final rehearsal:** A 90–100-minute continuous MP hold about three weeks out can be revealing for an athlete with substantial established history, with fueling and 48–72-hour recovery recorded. It is not a guaranteed race predictor. Scale down or omit if the cost would damage the taper. Preserve a real taper after the final major test.
- **Half transfer:** Orlando HMP may be a candidate MP band, not an automatic prescription. Revise MP from actual half outcome, conditions, HR/RPE, training load and recovery. Brice's observation that stable threshold is roughly 20–30 seconds per mile quicker than half capability pace for his sub-1:30 group is a **cohort hypothesis**, not a fixed physiological conversion or an excuse to force training pace.
- **Never author:** the prohibited program vocabulary, `3×10`-minute run workouts, or classic stopped mile repeats. Keep 2×10-minute threshold permissible. File total session time/distance, exact work/recovery segments, evidence and next-day cost, not just a poetic title.
