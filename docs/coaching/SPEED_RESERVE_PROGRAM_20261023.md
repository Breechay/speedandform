# Speed Reserve · FORM Running

**Name approved:** October 23, 2026, by Brice. **Program type:** Speed development, not a race entry or a marathon plan. **Publication status:** Draft source copy only; active Dossier and training-block names remain unchanged until a signed-in coach publishes and verifies them.

## Short line

Get faster over short distances, learn to hold that speed longer, and keep your endurance.

## App block description

Speed Reserve is a running development program for runners who want to become faster over the mile and 5K without giving up their endurance. We use short track repetitions to improve speed and coordination, longer repetitions and controlled sustained running to help that speed last, and easy long runs to maintain endurance. The group shares Thursday track workouts, while each runner has their own goals and long-run distances.

## What it means

Speed reserve means having more speed available than you need for a particular pace. Building that extra range can make the pace you want to sustain less demanding. But raw speed is only half the work: the plan also develops the ability to maintain it. The aim is repeatable faster running, not simply a quick interval or a hard session every day.

## Who it is for

For runners who already run consistently and want structured speed development for the mile, 5K, or faster everyday running. You do not need a race booked. New or returning runners need an individual review before receiving the same work. Kevin joins the shared Thursday track sessions but remains on his own first-marathon endurance plan.

## What training looks like

- **Thursday track:** the same session and targets for the current speed cohort. Short 60–200m work builds access to fast, coordinated running. Longer 400–1200m work develops repeatability. Controlled sustained efforts remain part of the progression. No decorative workout titles.
- **Monday iRUN:** join the actual group workout when posted. The on-calendar easy session is a placeholder, not a second workout to stack on top.
- **Saturday long run:** easy, individual distance. The speed cohort develops toward occasional ten- and twelve-mile outings where the coach confirms the overall weekly load supports them. Tinius sees kilometres.
- **Strength and easy running:** support the faster work without turning every day into another hard workout.
