## What the official FORM plan must prove

**Develop speed; sustain it; carry it farther; make it available late; arrive recovered enough to use it.** The official plan is a whole-athlete progression, not an ordered catalog of increasingly punishing long runs. Race-pace durability is its signature, with aerobic easy-running capacity, stable threshold, speed reserve, fueling and strength supporting the principal long-run question.

1. **Base:** Standalone 50–75-minute easy aerobic runs, as earned, and a comfortable two-hour long-run foundation before marathon-specific long-run work. Shorter true recovery runs remain allowed.
2. **Pace source:** Distinguish actual Orlando half pace, verified threshold training, and the *proposed* Donna MP. The pace is re-authored after the race, not mechanically inherited from a goal label.
3. **Pace continuity:** Use evidence of 30–45 → 45–55 → 60–70 min continuous MP, or comparable recovered race proof at the same appropriate pace. A 90-minute Orlando half at prospective Donna MP may already answer this narrow question.
4. **Pace re-entry:** Continuous MP/easy alternations, earned shortened transitions, explicit floats, and substantial MP blocks are options, not mandatory sequential workouts.
5. **Late availability:** The defining study question is whether evidenced pace is available after substantial preceding running, with fueling and an honest Closing-10K lens.
6. **Absorption:** The week owns a finite quality budget. A high-cost Saturday reduces another quality ask, strength cost, or both; no debt from missed workouts and no rush to replace evidence gaps.

Do not schedule all of these into a nine-week bridge. The plan chooses **one primary question per major week**, with cheaper surrounding work and recovery. The full double descending ladder is substantial, and a 90–100-minute MP rehearsal is high cost at a fast marathon pace.

## The key program-design decision: what should the final major test be?

The September 20 Plan 03 freeze protects a **Closing-10K late-access study**. Brice has now proposed an equally important **90–100-minute continuous MP rehearsal**. They answer different questions and cannot silently displace or stack with each other.

**Option A — preserve the Closing-10K experiment as principal (recommended provisional design).** If Orlando already supplies ~90 minutes at a credible proposed Donna clock pace, more continuous proof may be redundant; test that pace after longer prior running, fuel it, and maintain the frozen study's evidence continuity. A bounded continuous MP confirmation may replace other work if needed, not create an extra major peak.

**Option B — promote 90–100 minutes MP as the final principal rehearsal.** Date and document a formal amendment to the frozen sequence, preserving the original plan and rationale. Use only if recent training shows this work is absorbable and the result would change race-day decisions. Do **not** also require the original Closing-10K peak. Position the major rehearsal with **roughly 3–4 weeks** available before race day for absorption and taper; this is a coach-designed precaution informed by the broader evidence favoring disciplined tapers, not a validated universal 90–100-minute protocol.

**Option C — an individually reduced rehearsal.** If a PT/recovery or workload limit makes either principal test excessive, retain the scientific question using a smaller late-MP, easy/MP alternation or split-specific session. Never force a pass to complete a marketing story.

## Product, study and app acceptance

- **Coach authoring:** One canonical session family, objective, tier, exact work/recovery anatomy, pace source, work total, next-week cost, and coach-edited date/version. No automatic assignment from the method document.
- **Typed workout:** Six work reps and **five** between-rep transitions; distinguish easy vs float; preserve MP/HMP/10K/5K bands and exact units. A six-rep title must match work. A long-run version needs full easy approach and cooldown.
- **Athlete filing / Speed / feed:** Preserve planned and actual splits, cumulative pace time, longest *continuous* segment, float evidence, and only observed results. Do not publish invented splits or a false passed workout.
- **Study:** Preserve the September freeze and Last 10K hypotheses. Label the new 90–100-minute hypothesis and any change to the principal test as a dated amendment, never a backdated correction.
- **Commercial:** This is the first official FORM marathon *design*, not yet a validated self-guided product, launched checkout or proof of race outcomes. Product claims follow the actual study evidence.
