## A third undercount, already in the data

Recoveries are typed — `recovery_seconds` and `recovery_kind` — and nothing has
ever counted them. **A `jog`, `float` or `easy` recovery is running.** The four
jogs inside W9's `5 × 3 min` are 1.14 miles; The Kick's six 3-minute jogs are
1.71. A `standing` recovery is not running and stays uncounted.

Adding those, the thirty-three sessions in W3–W13 fall into three clean
populations, and only one of them needs a number changed:

**Already correct — 15 sessions.** Every long run and every easy day. The long
run *is* the whole session, so its declared distance was already the session
total. **Nothing to do.**

**`pd` is already the session total; the insides were never typed — 8 sessions.**
All named: Hills + strides, The Wave, The Blind Mile, The Governor, VO₂
intervals, Durability Read, Settle, VO₂ touch. *Hills + strides* declares five
miles and types three minutes of running. **The five is right.** These keep their
declared distance and get their warm-up, jog-downs and cool-down typed inside it
— between +1.0 and +4.3 miles of running that already existed and was invisible.
**No generic 20/10 stacked on top of any of them.**

**`pd` is the work only and must be raised — 10 sessions.** Every bare
`N mi at race pace`, and the three continuous race-pace Saturdays. `6 mi at race
pace` declares 6 and means 6 miles of race pace; the session is ~9.5. W3's
Tuesday is the proof — it declares 5 and carries a typed 20-minute warm-up and
10-minute cool-down on top, so its true session is 8.4.

Two sessions where the typed work already exceeds the declared total and the
number is simply wrong: **Pressure to Pace** (7.6 typed against 6) and
**The Kick** (6.4 against 6).

No hard piece is touched anywhere in this. No long run except W12's.

## The Labs instrument, noted for tranche C

**TOTAL** — every running mile expected that week, warm-ups included.

structured running. On this plan that gap runs 23 · 26 · 30 · 24 · 16 · 23 ·
27 · 27 · 24 · 29 · 25 — it widens through the ownership block and narrows at
the cutback, which is the training argument drawn without a word.
