STANDING FORM product law, Brice-confirmed from live-athlete feedback (Jose: "I like that we can change the easy runs / add distance+pace"). As the doctrine engine ([[form-programming-engine-v2]], [[form-v3-front-continuation]]) gets more authoritative, PROTECT athlete agency: FORM authors the purpose and the consequential structure; the athlete may negotiate the DOSE where changing it does not falsify that purpose. **Editable does not mean semantically unbounded.**

Model which parameters are **athlete-adjustable** vs **session-defining**:
- Easy/support → broadly editable (time, distance, optional pace).
- Converging Long `60 aerobic → 20 steady → 20 HM`: aerobic adjustable within a dose window; steady limited; **the decisive HM is protected**. `20 HM → 5 HM`, or dropping the 80-min preload and running HM fresh, is NO LONGER durability evidence.

FORM never scolds/prohibits — it records reality and changes EVIDENCE ELIGIBILITY ("still counts as training, but no longer resolves the planned durability read"). Athlete declares reality; FORM doesn't fake what happened.

**Why:** the smarter FORM gets, the more it must still feel like a coach working WITH you, not a spreadsheet locking you in.

**How to apply:** the seam already exists in the V3 typed work — `FORMV3SectionRole` (.preload/.decisive/.support) + the durability resolution (inside/outside/unmeasurable/notApplicable vs the decisive section; skipped/altered preload → notApplicable). Follow-on: expose per-section adjustability tiers at the Filing/edit surface and flip the durability read to not-eligible on an out-of-window edit of decisive/preload work — reuse this typing, do not add a parallel one.
