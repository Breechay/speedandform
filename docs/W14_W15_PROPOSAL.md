## Three things I want you to rule on

**1 · Should the race itself be eligible?** You said the Tuesday touch must not
move the ladder, and it won't. You didn't say anything about Saturday. I think it
should: 13.1 continuous inside 6:30–6:45 is the top rung, and if he runs it,
the thing the whole block was asking gets answered by the race answering it.
The alternative reading is that a race is a result, not evidence, and the ladder
should be moved by a coach's judgment rather than by a finish time.

**2 · Hope's Fridays.** She does not run four short easy days — she runs two long
ones. Her W1 was Mon 7.29 and Wed 7.27 and nothing else. So I have proposed
Friday **off** for her in both weeks, which is the only place I have differed
from José, and it costs her about 4 miles a week. Say if you would rather she
carried the same skeleton and we individualise later from evidence.

**3 · Hope goes under her band.** On 25 August she ran 6:29 · 6:20 · 6:22 · 6:19
against a `6:25–6:30` prescription — three of four under. Race week's `3 × 5 min`
is precisely where that costs something, because the whole point is finishing
wanting more. Her version can carry an explicit intent saying so — *"6:45 is the
target. Under 6:30 is not a better session, it is a different one, and this week
the different one costs you Saturday"* — or you write that line yourself.
