## Why the plan changed after Week 01

New evidence materially changed specificity, not the governing constraint:

- official marathon baseline supplied: **2:53:39 / 6:38 per mile**;
- athlete-reported near-term run build: **33 → 44 → 48 miles**;
- cable access confirmed;
- progress images show comparatively developed glutes and more room in chest depth, upper-arm girth, medial thigh and lower leg;
- pre-intervention running clips support preserving the existing elastic running identity rather than trying to rebuild it;
- athlete adherence / enjoyment is high enough to make a longitudinal study useful.

The response is **not** to increase punishment. Week 02 redirects the same four-day structure toward more valuable tissue and keeps lower-body progression subordinate to running response.

## Study standard

The study asks:

> Can a 2:53 marathoner build a visibly stronger, more substantial body without losing the qualities that already make him fast?

Evidence is split into four channels:
- body;
- strength;
- running;
- athlete experience.

No body-mass gain, circumference gain, hypertrophy result or causal success is claimed before measurement exists.
