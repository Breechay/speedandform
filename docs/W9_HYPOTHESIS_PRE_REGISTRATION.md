# W9 Hypothesis — Pre-registered Experiment

**Created:** 5 September 2026  
**Status:** Pre-registered before any athlete reaches W9  
**Purpose:** Establish W9 as the first canonical plan week where the distinction between "execution established" and "durability corroborated" becomes concrete enough to build against.

---

## Background

W9 is the first week in Race Pace Durability that asks **two different questions** about race-pace capability:

**Tuesday asks:** *Can you carry 8 continuous miles at the authored RP without the cost materially rising?*

**Saturday asks:** *Can you access RP again late in the week and late in a long run after Tuesday already happened?*

These should generate separate evidence. A single pass/fail threshold cannot capture this distinction, and describing what happened afterward would be post-hoc rationalization rather than experiment.

---

## Hypothesis

An athlete who has established 8 continuous miles at RP should be able to re-access RP four days later for the final 4 miles of a 17-mile long run without material degradation.

---

## Pre-registered Evidence Framework

### Tuesday: Continuous Capability

**Session:** 8 mi continuous @ 6:30–6:45 (W9 Tuesday)

**Raw evidence collected:**
- 8 continuous miles completed (no material breaks)
- Mile/lap splits
- HR trace if trustworthy
- RPE progression
- Interruptions / environmental context (heat, terrain, etc.)

**Coach interpretation:**
- Cost rising or stable
- Mechanical compensation present or absent
- Capability supported
- Capability not supported
- Insufficient evidence

**Interpretation framework:**
- The continuous 8-mile effort is carried within the authored race-pace band without material loss of control. Splits, GPS quality, and context support the verdict — one mile reading 6:46 due to GPS noise or a turn does not constitute failure.
- HR decoupling is a leading indicator but **not the verdict** — heat, hydration, sensor quality, and normal cardiac drift can make a clean run look dirty
- HR corroborates the performance; it does not define it
- First-half vs second-half efficiency is a useful quantitative secondary measure — if 6:35 pace early costs X HR and the same pace late costs substantially more, the athlete completed the task but the cost rose

**Tuesday supports continuous capability when:**
- 8 mi are completed continuously
- The effort is carried within the authored band without material loss of control
- No meaningful late-session pace collapse
- Physiological/mechanical cost does not show obvious compensation

### Saturday: Durability Under Compounding Fatigue

**Session:** 17 mi long run, last 4 @ RP (W9 Saturday)

**Raw evidence collected:**
- 4 finishing miles completed continuously
- Mile/lap splits for the finishing miles
- Whether the athlete had to abandon or materially break the continuous finish
- Pace deterioration across those four miles (first RP mile vs last RP mile)
- HR trace if trustworthy
- RPE progression
- Interruptions / environmental context

**Coach interpretation:**
- Cost rising or stable
- Mechanical compensation present or absent
- Durability under compounding fatigue supported
- Durability under compounding fatigue not supported
- Insufficient evidence

**Saturday supports durability under compounding fatigue when:**
- All 4 finishing miles are completed continuously
- The effort is carried within the authored band without material loss of control
- The final miles do not materially deteriorate relative to the first RP mile

## Possible Results

| Tuesday | Saturday | Interpretation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| Supports | Supports | Capability corroborated under fatigue |
| Supports | Does not support | Isolated capability exists; repeatability/durability not yet established |
| Does not support | Any | 8-mile continuous capability remains unestablished. Saturday may still provide separate durability/context evidence but cannot establish the Tuesday claim retroactively |
| Contextual disruption | Any | Insufficient evidence rather than automatic failure (heat, illness, etc.) |

---

## Fractional Credit: The Key Innovation

This framework makes concrete the distinction that has been theoretical:

**Execution established** — Tuesday evidence supports continuous capability  
**Durability corroborated** — Saturday evidence supports durability under compounding fatigue

An athlete can have:

- **execution established**  
- **but durability not corroborated**

This is the fractional-credit case the product has been looking for. W9 is the first canonical plan week where this distinction becomes concrete enough to build against rather than theorize about.

---

## Deliberate Conservatism on Numerical Bars

We do **not** immediately decree precise numerical thresholds such as:
- ">5 bpm drift = failure"
- "last mile >5 sec slow = failure"

That would be fake precision. Better to:

1. Establish the primary rule from the authored pace band first
2. Let HR, RPE, and within-session drift explain *how* it was achieved
3. If future numerical thresholds are adopted, they must be supported by repeated field evidence and then prospectively registered before being used for verdicts

The distinction between "completed but cost rose" vs "did not complete" is precisely different from a binary pass/fail.

---

## Implementation Notes

This pre-registration is deliberately conservative about exact numerical thresholds at first. The framework establishes:

1. What evidence to collect
2. How to interpret it
3. What outcomes are possible
4. Which outcomes constitute fractional credit

It does **not** pre-specify the exact numerical boundaries that would trigger each outcome. Any future numerical thresholds must be supported by repeated field evidence and then prospectively registered before being used for verdicts.
