## What Labs is, in one line each

- **Bench** `#/bench` — one column per athlete. Takes a position: verdict first,
  then LAST KEY with its prescription and what came back, NEXT KEY chosen by
  weight rather than date, then the instrument against its target.
- **Brief** `#/brief` — five messages you have not sent yet. Ends in an ask.
- **Plan** `#/a/:slug/block` — the block as a matrix. Weeks across, days down,
  no cell borders, the easy budget on its own row, weekly volume beneath.
- **Read** — the only verb wired. Writes a coach read and clears the item.
- The Console lives at `/coach/console/` and can author typed anatomy.

## The corrections this stream made, so they are not resurrected

- The ladder reports evidence. **It never rewrites the plan.**
- Owned is the longest single continuous segment held in band, derived from
  filed pieces. Not the sum of broken work. Not typed by hand.
- `is_key` currently means "has a day". The easy budget has no day and is not key.
- A rung is not just another key session — say when the next one moves the ladder.
- An athlete is not an app user. `delivery` says which, and a coach-delivered
  athlete with no membership is a mode, not a gap.
- Blank means unchanged. Silence beats filler.
- RLS answers "what may I read", never "who am I".
