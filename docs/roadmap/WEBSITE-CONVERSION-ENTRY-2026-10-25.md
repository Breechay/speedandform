# Website · The stranger's first visit

October 25, 2026 · Owner: Speed & Form · Working release branch `codex/conversion-entry-20261025`

## The job

Someone who did not come from a friend should understand three things before deciding whether to contact Brice:

1. Who is coaching them, and what his attention is like.
2. What the work looks like and where real results can be inspected.
3. Whether they should ask about coaching, try a plan, or join a confirmed Thursday.

The homepage should remain a photographic editorial experience. We do not need a crowded sales-template landing page or unverified praise.

## Release 02 · Human evidence

- Request short, explicit testimonial permission from three to four athletes. Ask what they wanted, what changed, and whether their name/photo/result may be published. No invented quotes, percentages or outcome guarantees.
- Use the existing athlete dossiers and lab studies as the deeper evidence destination. Fix a concrete broken athlete link only if a repeatable browser case reproduces it; current source already has actual athlete destinations.
- Keep homepage proof to one or two specific, dated achievements with a link to the underlying study. A training result is labeled as a training result, not a promised race outcome.
- Give each permissioned story one real photo, one short quote, one measurable before/after where records exist, and an athlete-approved framing.

## Release 03 · Organic discovery and measurement

- Audit Google Search Console indexing, queries and landing pages, especially Miami running coach, half-marathon training plans, Run Development and Thursday. Verify rather than guess which searches currently bring people.
- Check page titles/descriptions, canonical destinations, structured data, mobile performance, internal links and image sizing. Preserve authored share imagery and actual athlete evidence.
- Define a small journey: search landing → training path → application started → accepted inquiry → qualified conversation. Confirm analytics events, attribution carryover, and privacy behavior before restarting search ads.
- Make a monthly report of search impressions/clicks, offer-path clicks, accepted inquiries and booked paying athletes. Direction matters more than vanity traffic.
