The training is considered.
Everything around it should be too.

No changing in an Uber. No rental-car key on a tire. No looking for a bathroom with eighteen miles still ahead of you.

5:30. The city is still dark. The House is not. Your bag goes into a locker. A fresh towel is there. The route is up. Someone else is here for the same morning.

You warm up properly. Then you step outside. Bridge. Water. Pavement. The city stays the run. The House does not move it indoors.

You finish at the door.

MeetA dependable early door. Bags down. People know where to meet.
TrainThe session stays where it belongs: outside, on the track, road, trail, bridge or boardwalk.
RecoverWater, mobility, somewhere to sit, and showers where the host can support them.
FuelCoffee, breakfast or simple post-training food. Fuel belongs in the morning too.
BelongThe people and the practice have somewhere to return to. Same door next time.
Why now

The behavior already exists.
The infrastructure is catching up.

Strava reported running-club creation up 3.5× in 2025 and club-organized events up 1.5×. Its prior research found social connection was the top reason 48% of respondents joined a fitness group. FORM House is a proposal for the physical layer around that real-world behavior.
