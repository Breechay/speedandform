The run is only part of the morning.

It is 5:50 AM. Your bag is down. Coffee is already on. Someone is tying a shoe. Someone else is checking the route.

At six, everyone leaves. Some are running easy. Some have work to do. The city is the training ground.

Then everyone comes back somewhere. Water. Food. A shower if there is one. Ten minutes on the floor. Twenty minutes talking. The morning has somewhere to land.

That is FORM House.

Why now

Running is becoming a place people go to find people.

The sport is growing, but the more interesting shift is cultural: runners are not only signing up for races. They are gathering around movement.

Strava reported that new running clubs grew 3.5× in 2025 and club-organized events rose 1.5× year over year. U.S. race participation also kept growing in 2025. The demand is already visible. FORM House asks a simple question: where does that energy go before and after the run?

Start small

Give us a few mornings. See what the place becomes.

  1. Open the space before the run.
  2. Give runners somewhere for bags, water and bathrooms.
  3. Let FORM host the training.
  4. Welcome everyone back for coffee, food or recovery.
  5. Watch who returns, how long they stay and whether the space feels more alive because the practice is there.
The first version does not need to be perfect.

It needs to make a 6 AM training morning noticeably better. If people keep coming back, we will know what deserves to be built next.
