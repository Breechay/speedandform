The idea

The training is considered.
The return should be too.

A place to arrive early, leave for your run and come back to a morning worth staying for.

Your bag is where you left it. A clean towel and cold drink are ready. Breakfast is generous, the seat is comfortable and there is time to enjoy the people around you.

The run stays outside. The House gives it a home.

01 · Train

Your miles.

Road, track, bridge or waterfront. Run the distance and effort that fit your training, with people to head out alongside.

02 · Recover

A good return.

Towel off. Find shade and a comfortable seat. Showers and changing space are optional when available.

03 · Fuel

A proper breakfast.

Cold drinks, fruit and food ready as runners return. A substantial bowl, shake or savory plate. Good coffee at the table.

04 · Belong

Room to stay.

Bring a friend for breakfast. Talk, read, work or put your feet up. Coming just for the table belongs here too.
