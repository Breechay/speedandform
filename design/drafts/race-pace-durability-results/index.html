Race Pace Durability · 15 weeks

Make race pacefamiliar.

The goal is not to visit race pace once. It is to stop treating it like a special event.

Assign a realistic pace. Touch it repeatedly. Keep some speed above it. Build enough endurance underneath it. Then sharpen the work until the target pace can survive the distance.

02 · StartDo not invent a laboratory baseline.

The starting point was simple.

They had race fitness. They did not have a history of specifically owning the new target pace for long uninterrupted distances.

The useful question was not “what was their perfect threshold number?” It was: what pace do we think they can grow into, and how do we make that pace increasingly normal?

03 · MethodSequence the adaptations.

Acclimate. Build. Sharpen.

Race pace becomes useful when the rest of the training makes it cheaper, more familiar and more durable.

The method is the sequencing: raise the ceiling here and there, build endurance and durability underneath, keep exposing the athlete to race pace, then sharpen with increasing specificity.

01 · AssignChoose the promise.

Set a realistic athlete-relative race-pace band. It is a hypothesis, not a trophy pace.

02 · AcclimateSee it often.

Broken race-pace work creates repeated exposure without demanding full race durability on day one.

03 · SupportBuild around it.

Easy volume and long runs build the floor. Threshold and occasional higher-ceiling work preserve room above the target.

04 · SharpenRemove the escapes.

Recovery shrinks, continuous ownership grows, and race pace appears later in long runs until the work resembles the demand.

04 · DecisionsThe schedule is not the method by itself.

Progress only when the evidence earns it.

The plan teaches what a successful exposure means and when the athlete needs more time at the same demand.

Held the band

Keep building familiarity.

A clean execution supports the next step when the effort and late-race cost remain controlled.

Drifted late

The pace is not ordinary yet.

Do not automatically move forward just because the distance was completed. Repeat or consolidate the demand.

Could not hold

Interpret the miss.

Weather, pacing, fatigue, fueling and fitness are different problems. The response should match the reason.

Missed training

Return to what is current.

No apology mileage. Re-establish the athlete's current state and resume the sequence from the appropriate point.
