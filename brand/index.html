How we decide

Ask these in order.

01What is the real object?A pattern is what you reach for before you have found it. The track was already in the photograph.
02Can you name every number?If a line can’t say what it measures, it is decoration. Take the geometry from the real thing.
03What does it line up with?Everything sits on an edge something else already uses.
04What does it replace?If nothing comes out, nothing goes in.
05Is it one thing?One accessory per view. One bend per piece.
06Is it found, not announced?Quiet from across the room. Clear once you notice it. Lines never cross type.
07Does the photograph still have the room?The photograph is the luxury. Everything else is the room that receives it.

Light

Lit, not printed.

RuleThe photograph keeps the brightest point.On black, a headline starts at full strength and falls to 60% by its far end. The emblem does the same.
Stays fullAnything you read to decide.Body copy, prices, dates, links.
NeverEffects on letters.No texture, bevel, glow or shadow. Richness comes from real material, photographed.
