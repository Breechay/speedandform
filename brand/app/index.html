Material behavior

The differences are intentional.

Quiet Clay

Bone canvas, chalk-white Filing inserts, charcoal Save Run. The current week sits on a brighter chalk card, without a side marker.

Stronger Signature

The selected week fills with clay. Effort, focus and Save Run use a stronger clay accent. No side marker.

After Dark

Warm charcoal layers and fine outlines. Sunset highlights the selected effort; the primary action turns chalk. No side marker.

Shared type, spacing, navigation and coaching data remain unchanged across themes. The site itself keeps the separate black, graphite and bone house palette.
