02 / THE PRACTICE

I develop runners.

Training is an argument made over time.
I assess where you are, prescribe the work,
observe what it establishes, and decide what comes next.

I watch for ease.

Foot landing, arm swing, the rhythm between them. Never one thing at a time. What I am reading is whether the whole stride agrees with itself, and speed is what shows up when it does.

You are an accumulation.

No session stands by itself. Each one is written on top of the last, and that order carries most of the effect. Swap one and the running still happened. The thread I was reading did not.

I want the quiet weeks.

Fresh, fed, slept, moving well. That is the week worth having, not the heroic one. Stack eight of them and the same paces cost less. More ground, less negotiation.
