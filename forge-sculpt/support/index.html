Restore a purchase

Open FORGE, go to the membership screen, and select Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Manage or cancel a subscription

FORGE memberships are billed through Apple. You can manage or cancel anytime from your iPhone: Settings, then your Apple ID, then Subscriptions.

Delete your training data

FORGE does not create an account. Your training data is stored on your device, and you can permanently remove it from inside the app: open Profile → Your training → Delete training data and confirm. Your first name, membership entitlement, and Athlete Key access remain. If you have a question about what this removes, email us.

Basic troubleshooting

If something looks wrong, close and reopen FORGE, confirm you are on the latest version from the App Store, and restart your device if needed. If the issue continues, email support with what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead.
