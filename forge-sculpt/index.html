The commitment

Know the work
before you begin.

Sculpt is for experienced lifters who want a directed physique program, not a blank workout tracker.

A full gym
Barbells, dumbbells, cables, and machines. This is not a home-dumbbell plan.
Time for the week
The opening week has six sessions. Later schedules follow the phase. Check the actual week in the app.
Movement experience
You should already be able to use the equipment and perform the movements safely. This is not a beginner’s lifting course.
A written direction
The sessions are authored, not generated on demand. You choose your loads; the program gives the work.

Before you start

A few useful answers.

Is this the Forge app?

Yes. Forge was the project name. The app is Speed & Form: Strength, and Breechay Sculpt is the year of training inside it. The button on this page opens it in the App Store.

What happens if I miss or shorten a workout?

The app keeps the sets you finished. The next session waits for you, so you pick up where you left off. Nothing is marked done that you did not do.

Can Brice write the program for me?

Yes, that is Strength coaching: eight weeks, eight coached sessions in Miami, and your plan loaded into the app. Sculpt on its own is the written year, no coaching. See Strength coaching.

Is there an Android or iPad version?

Not yet. It is built for iPhone and needs iOS 17 or later. The App Store shows compatibility for your device.

What if a movement is painful or unfamiliar?

Do not push through pain to complete the program. Stop and get appropriate help with the movement or symptoms. An app cannot assess an injury or replace in-person instruction.

Where do I get help?

App support covers app questions. Privacy and terms remain available before you download.
