The commitment

Know the work
before you begin.

Sculpt is for experienced lifters who want a directed physique program, not a blank workout tracker.

A full gym
Barbells, dumbbells, cables, and machines. This is not a home-dumbbell plan.
Time for the week
The opening week has six sessions. Later schedules follow the phase. Check the actual week in the app.
Movement experience
You should already be able to use the equipment and perform the movements safely. This is not a beginner’s lifting course.
A written direction
The sessions are authored, not generated on demand. You choose your loads; the program gives the work.

Before you start

A few useful answers.

Is this the Forge app?

The App Store name is Breechay. Sculpt is the physique program. Forge is the name used in the project and earlier website material. The button on this page opens the current Breechay listing.

What happens if I miss or shorten a workout?

Record the work you actually did. An incomplete session is not a completed one. A change of training position does not create completed workouts.

Does this include personal coaching?

No. Sculpt is a written program delivered through Breechay. A personal coaching relationship has its own setup. Talk with Brice about individual coaching.

Is there an Android or iPad version?

The current Breechay listing is designed for iPhone and requires iOS 17 or later. Check the App Store compatibility information for your device. This page does not promise an Android or dedicated iPad app.

What if a movement is painful or unfamiliar?

Do not push through pain to complete the program. Stop and get appropriate help with the movement or symptoms. An app cannot assess an injury or replace in-person instruction.

Where do I get help?

Breechay support covers app questions. Privacy and terms remain available before you download.
