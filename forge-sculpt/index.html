Before starting Sculpt

Does it fit
your training?

Sculpt is for experienced lifters who want to build muscle.

A full gym
Access to barbells, dumbbells, cables, and machines.
Six opening workouts
Six sessions in the opening week. Later schedules change with the phase.
Lifting experience
You should already know how to use the equipment and perform the movements safely.
Your working weights
The program gives you the sets and reps. You choose the weight you can lift with control.

Before you start

Questions?

Is this the Forge app?

Yes. It is now called Speed & Form: Strength. Breechay Sculpt is one of the programs inside it.

What if I finish only part of a workout?

The app keeps the sets you completed, so you can still look back at what you lifted.

Can Brice write the program for me?

Yes. Strength coaching includes eight coached sessions in Miami over eight weeks, plus your own training plan in the app. The Sculpt subscription covers the written Sculpt program. Coaching is separate. See Strength coaching.

Is there an Android or iPad version?

The app is built for iPhone and needs iOS 17 or later. The App Store shows compatibility for your device.

What if a movement is painful or unfamiliar?

Do not push through pain to complete the program. Stop and get appropriate help with the movement or symptoms. An app cannot assess an injury or replace in-person instruction.

Where do I get help?

App support covers app questions. Privacy and terms remain available before you download.
