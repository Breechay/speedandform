Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 15, 2026

This policy explains what information FORGE handles, how it is stored, and your choices. FORGE is a standalone iOS app published by Brice Ikouebe, operating as Speed & Form.

Local-first. FORGE does not require an account or login, and does not run its own servers that store your training. Your work is kept on your device, and you can create an exported backup file whenever you choose. The separate Speed & Form coach web tools are a different product and are not the FORGE app's backend.

01

Who we are

FORGE is published by Brice Ikouebe, operating as Speed & Form, based in Florida, United States. You can reach us at support@speedandform.com.

02

No account required

FORGE does not require you to create an account or sign in to train. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, or phone number to use the app.

03

Your training data

FORGE stores your training information locally on your device. This includes, where used: your recorded training sessions, an active workout, your program, phase, week, and day position, program lifecycle and run history, skipped days and progression records, exercise substitutions, body and waist logs, intake or accountability logs, progress photos, and your training preferences.

Backup. FORGE includes an Export FORGE backup action in Profile → Your training. The exported file is created on your device and stays wherever you choose to save it. FORGE does not run cloud backup or automatic synchronization for your training data.

04

Purchases and membership

FORGE memberships are sold as Apple in-app purchases. Apple processes all payments. We never receive or store your card number or full payment details. FORGE receives only the transaction and entitlement status from Apple needed to unlock and maintain your membership. Apple may retain its own purchase and subscription records, governed by Apple's terms, separately from FORGE.

05

Analytics and tracking

FORGE does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking identifiers, and does not use third-party crash-reporting services. FORGE does not track you across other apps or websites.

06

Diagnostics and analytics

The standalone FORGE app does not use Apple MetricKit diagnostics, and does not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services. Apple may collect device and diagnostic information under your own Apple settings, governed by Apple's privacy policy.

07

Notifications

FORGE may offer optional, on-device reminders for certain programs, including Rod Accountability protein reminders. These reminders are scheduled locally on your device after you grant permission. FORGE does not use a push-notification token for these reminders, the reminder information is not sent to the developer, and notifications are never required for core training.

08

Third-party services

Within the FORGE app, the only third party involved in normal use is Apple, which processes in-app purchases. Apple's handling of that information is governed by its privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

09

Selling of personal information

FORGE does not sell your personal information.

10

Deleting your training data

You can permanently remove your FORGE training data from this device at any time. Open Profile → Your training → Delete training data. After a destructive confirmation, this removes your recorded sessions, active workout, program position, training history, preferences, intake logs, and progress photos from the device. Your first name, your membership or purchase entitlement, and your Athlete Key access remain.

This is training-data deletion, not account deletion or purchase cancellation — FORGE has no account, and purchases are managed through Apple. Deleting the app also removes its locally stored data from the device, but the in-app control above is the intended path. An exported backup file you created earlier remains wherever you saved it until you delete that file. Apple's purchase records remain with Apple.

11

Children's privacy

FORGE is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you are under the age of majority where you live, use FORGE only with the involvement and permission of a parent or legal guardian.

12

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will revise the effective date above. Continued use of FORGE after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@speedandform.com.

14

The website launch form

This section covers the speedandform.com website, not the FORGE app. If you choose to enter your email address in the optional launch-notification form on the FORGE page, we collect that email address for one purpose: to send you a single notification when FORGE is available on the App Store.

The form is processed and stored by Netlify (our website host) through Netlify Forms, governed by Netlify's privacy policy. Your address is not added to any other mailing list, is not shared or sold, and is not used for anything beyond the promised launch message. We delete the collected addresses after the launch notification is sent. To have your address removed sooner, email support@speedandform.com.

The FORGE app itself does not require or collect an email address to train.
