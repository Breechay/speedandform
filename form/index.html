The idea behind FORM

Most runners do too much of the wrong running.

  • Hard days too easy. Easy days too hard.
  • Everything at the same volume, adding up to nothing.
  • FORM sets the emphasis — each run does its job, and the week compounds.

Questions

Before you begin.

Do I need to build my own plan?+

No. FORM arranges the week for you — every kind of running in relationship. You open the app and run what belongs today.

What races does FORM support?+

FORM is built around campaigns like the half marathon and marathon, with paces and a week that progress toward a goal. More on the specific campaigns is shown inside the app.

Does it work with my watch and shoes?+

FORM shows the session, the paces, and the shape of the week; you run it with whatever watch and shoes you use. Logging keeps your record in one place.

What is "the evidence"?+

FORM reads your completed work and explains meaningful changes when there is enough evidence. When there is not, it says so plainly rather than inventing a story.

Is there a team or social side?+

FORM includes Your Village — a quiet, work-first way to train alongside a group. It is built around standards and shared work, not leaderboards.
