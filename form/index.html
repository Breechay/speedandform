A simple loop

See it. Run it.
Keep the record.

Each run belongs to a week. Each week belongs to something you’re working toward.

  1. 01

    Open today.

    See the work in front of you, with its pace or effort and its place in the plan.

  2. 02

    Know the whole session.

    Read the warm-up, main work, recoveries, and finish before you head out.

  3. 03

    Record what happened.

    Keep what you ran, not just what you intended to run. Come back to the record when you need it.

Five kinds of running

Different work.
A clear reason for each.

Easy, Threshold, Interval, Speed, and Long Run. Explore what each kind of session is there to develop.

Easy

Keep it comfortable.

Build the habit and the aerobic work you can return to. This is running you can hold a conversation through.

Read about easy running →

Conversational effort

Room to run again

Threshold

Strong. Controlled. Repeatable.

Sustained work at a demanding but controlled effort. The session gives the duration, pace or effort, and any recovery.

Understand threshold training →

Controlled sustained work

Not an all-out time trial

Interval

Work. Recover. Go again.

Repetitions with a defined recovery between them. Read the effort and the recovery together; both belong to the session.

Measured repetitions

Recovery is part of the work

Speed

Short work. Clear intent.

Brief, quicker efforts with recovery. The goal is to execute the intended work, not to turn every repetition into a race.

Long Run

Keep running well, longer.

A longer time on your feet. Some sessions stay easy; others include a deliberately steadier finish. Follow the purpose of the day.

Before you begin

A few useful answers.

Do I need to be coached by Brice?

No. You can follow the plans in the app on your own. Coaching starts only when we both agree to it. Ask me about it.

How does my plan get into the app?

If I coach you, your assigned plan appears when you sign in. The plans already in the app, you choose yourself.

Which races is the app for?

The 5K, 10K, half marathon and marathon. Each plan shows what it asks of you before you start. For HYROX running, see the HYROX guide.

Is the dark screen what the app looks like now?

It is the new design, with an example run. The session-details image is from the App Store listing, which shows the version you can download today.

What does it cost?

The app is free to download. Written plans and coaching are priced on their own pages. The Library is free.

What do I need to use it?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later, or an iPad with iPadOS 17 or later. You can review current compatibility in the App Store. For setup or a connection question, start with app support.
