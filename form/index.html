Before you begin

A few useful answers.

Do I need to be coached by Brice?

No. You can follow a program in the app on your own. Personal coaching is a separate service. Ask me about it.

How does my plan get into the app?

If I coach you, your assigned plan appears when you sign in. Otherwise, choose a program in the app.

How do I choose a plan?

Check the available programs and what each asks of you before you start. Choose one that fits your goal and the weeks you can commit to. For HYROX running, see the HYROX guide.

What does it cost?

The app is free to download. Written plans and coaching are priced on their own pages. The Library is free.

What do I need to use it?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later, or an iPad with iPadOS 17 or later. You can review current compatibility in the App Store. For setup or a connection question, start with app support.
