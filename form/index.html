    Open today.

    Record what happened.

    Keep what you ran, not just what you intended to run. Come back to the record when you need it.

Before you begin

A few useful answers.

Do I need to be coached by Brice?

No. FORM is also for runners training independently. Downloading the app does not start a personal coaching relationship. Coaching has its own inquiry and setup.

Which races is FORM for?

The current App Store listing describes half-marathon, marathon, and HYROX running. Check the available programs and their requirements in the app. For the broader HYROX course and station tools, visit the website guide.

Is the dark screen the current App Store version?

The dark illustration previews the new interface, using example training. The session-details image is from the public App Store listing. The App Store shows the version available to download.

What does access cost?

Current download and access options are shown in FORM and the App Store. Published training plans and personal coaching have their own terms. No app subscription or plan purchase is required to read the Library.

What do I need to use it?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later, or an iPad with iPadOS 17 or later. You can review current compatibility in the App Store. For setup or a connection question, start with FORM support.
