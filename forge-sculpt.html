Breechay Sculpt

Four phases.
One complete silhouette.

Each phase changes the emphasis while preserving the work that came before it. Breechay Sculpt is one continuous program, not four unrelated plans.

IPhase
Build the Frame

The first phase emphasizes the upper-body silhouette: shoulders, upper chest, back, and arms. The objective is width, structure, and a stronger visual frame.

IIPhase
Glute Build

The second phase gives the lower body and glutes greater emphasis while continuing to support the physique, developing the lower silhouette without abandoning the frame established in Phase 1.

IIIPhase
Specialize

The third phase develops the areas that most influence the completed silhouette, with added attention to glute development, core, proportion, and refinement.

IVPhase
Reveal

The fourth phase is built to preserve the physique while helping reveal the work completed across the program.

The emphasis moves. The work accumulates.
