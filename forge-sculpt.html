Breechay Sculpt

Four phases.
One complete silhouette.

Each phase changes the emphasis while preserving the work that came before it. Breechay Sculpt is one continuous program for the masculine frame — not four unrelated plans.

IPhase
Build the Frame

Shoulders, upper chest, back, and arms establish the upper silhouette. The objective is width, structure, and a stronger visual frame.

IIPhase
Glute Build

Lower-body emphasis arrives without abandoning the frame — developing the glutes and posterior chain while the upper work is maintained.

IIIPhase
Specialize

Glutes, core, arms, and proportion. The phase develops the areas that most influence the completed silhouette.

IVPhase
Reveal

Preserve the structure while bringing the completed silhouette forward — holding the work built across the program.

The emphasis moves. The work accumulates.

Questions

Before you begin.

Can I use my own weights?+

Yes. The work is prescribed; the loads stay appropriate to your current ability. You choose the weight for each set.

Is this only for advanced lifters?+

No. You should be comfortable training on your own and selecting safe loads, but you do not need to be advanced. The program handles the structure.

Do I have to build a routine?+

No. That is the entire point. The week is arranged and every session is written before you arrive.

Is Week 1 really free?+

Yes. You can train through the opening week before deciding. Membership begins only when you continue beyond Week 1.

Can I restore my purchase?+

Yes, through Apple's purchase restoration, from the membership screen in FORGE. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID used originally.
