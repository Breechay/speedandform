A few practical questions.

Know what you are choosing before you commit.

Will you come to my building gym?

Where the location, equipment and building rules make sense, yes. Share the neighborhood and gym name first. Travel and trainer access must be confirmed before payment.

Do I need to be a runner?

No. This is standalone strength coaching. If running is also a priority, Run + Strength combines running coaching with strength programming and four coached gym sessions for $1,800 over eight weeks.

What if I am new to lifting?

That is a good reason to have coaching. We begin with movements and loads you can practice confidently and progress from there.

What happens if a session needs to move?

Give at least 24 hours’ notice to reschedule. Late cancellations normally count as a used session. We agree on dates and any planned time away before starting; a session I cancel is rescheduled or refunded.

Does this include nutrition or rehabilitation?

The offer covers strength training and its programming. It does not include medical treatment, physical therapy or a prescribed nutrition plan. Tell me if another professional has given you training restrictions; detailed health records do not belong in this form.
