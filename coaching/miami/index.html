The week

Assign.

Paces, cues, and the work for the week. Enough direction to know what the session is asking for without turning every run into a test.

Meet.

One private 45–60 minute running session each week in Miami, outdoors. We choose a practical place after I know your neighborhood. There is no promised citywide travel radius.

Adjust.

Load, recovery, and the next week change from what the work shows. One useful change, then we watch the next rep.
