The question

What could FORM make possible in an athlete's life that probably would not have happened, or would have been much harder, without FORM?

Every idea for the app, the team and the coaching starts here. Running better is part of the answer. So are the people you meet, the places you go, the time you stop spending on logistics, and a history worth keeping.

The answer may not be another feature. It may be a better morning, an introduction, a photograph or one clear decision.

What FORM is

FORM is a running practice in Miami and the people who train in it, near and far.

A coach writes the training for each person. Thursday track and long runs bring people together. Photographs and film keep the history. The app carries all of it.

The app is not the whole product. It is the surface of a service, and it should feel like one person remembered every detail.

FORM stays small on purpose. We would rather be extraordinarily valuable to the people who choose it than be big.

Who it is for

  • Coached athletes who want to open the app and know exactly what to do.
  • Remote athletes who train far from Miami and still belong to the team.
  • Thursday regulars who need the time, the place and the workout.
  • Long run people who want to know how far, where and who is going.
  • Racers who are counting down to a day that matters.
  • Independent athletes who have their own plan and come for the people.
  • New people who heard about a run and want to see what this is.

Most people are more than one of these, and it changes through a season.

What people need from us

Know what to do

Open the app, see the work, do it, file it. No decoding and no extra taps. Plans move when life moves.

Get better, and see it

Results matter. So does understanding why. A good week should make sense, and a bad one should too.

Have people to run with

Familiar faces make the early alarm easier. Post a run, say I'm in, and someone goes with you. Company stays a choice.

Feel remembered

A specific question, an honest observation, a photograph from a day that mattered. Care comes from relevance, not volume.

Go somewhere new

A race in another city, a long run off the usual routes, one door you always wanted to open.

Keep the history

Every run, photo and race stays. A season should be worth coming back to.

Have a way back

People get hurt, get busy and change goals. Coming back should be simple, and nobody should have to explain themselves twice.

How we decide

Start with a real person and something they want. Then ask what the best answer is: a conversation, an introduction, a morning, a photograph or software.

Try the smallest real version. Watch what people do, not what they say they might do. Keep what works and drop what does not.

Build at Apple-level ease. If something saves five minutes and takes ten to explain, it is wrong. If a screen needs a paragraph, the screen needs work.

What we protect

  • Your data stays yours. Training, health, location and notes stay private unless you choose to share them.
  • Connection is a choice. Nobody's schedule, home, goals or travel is exposed because they joined.
  • The coach is the coach. A group run never replaces your plan. AI never rewrites it.
  • No manufactured engagement. No streak pressure, guilt, rankings or bait.
  • The relationship stays real. Automated messages never pretend to be a person noticing you.
  • FORM works without the app. The people and the service do not disappear if you use other tools.
  • Nothing is claimed before it is real. A plan, a feature or a result counts when it exists.
