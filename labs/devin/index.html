Cut without
shrinking.

Can Devin get leaner while keeping the strength work normal and making easy running a useful support rather than another thing to chase?

The question

Preserve the frame while the body changes.

This is a deliberately small study. We do not need a grand page to coach the question well.

The useful read is whether strength remains available, easy running stays cheap, the week becomes repeatable, and comparable body evidence moves in the intended direction.

More is not the score. More cardio, a longer fast or a harder easy run does not automatically improve the cut.
What we watch
Practice

Did the week happen?

Strength attendance, easy runs, schedule friction and unplanned extras.

Capacity

Do the lifts stay normal?

Comparable load, reps, RIR and movement quality when recorded.

Body

Is the direction visible?

Only comparable weight, waist, photos or silhouette evidence that Devin actually supplies.

Current rule

Easy running supports the lift.

The existing Week 01 page used three 35-minute conversational runs around four lifting days. That is a baseline structure, not an obligation to keep increasing cardio.

If added running makes the strength flat or the week harder to repeat, the aerobic support gets cheaper first. Missed support is not debt.
