The problem

He will do exactly what you give him.

Tinius does not need more motivation. He needs the right amount of work in the right places. The running program already asks real questions of him, so a physique plan cannot behave as if the legs are starting from zero.

The target is familiar: broader shoulders, stronger upper back and upper chest, a sharper waist, and enough unilateral/posterior-chain strength to support the runner rather than compete with him.

Athlete consent for this public study was confirmed before publication. Training observations are coaching evidence, not medical claims.
The integration

Three strength days. None of them gets to steal a run.

Tuesday

Frame A

Upper-dominant after FORM quality: shoulders, upper chest and back. Minimal leg fatigue.

Thursday

Lower + Core

Unilateral leg strength, posterior chain and trunk only when Tuesday is absorbed. If FORM Thursday is already a real quality session, use trunk/calf support or skip the lower-body dose. Saturday stays available.

Sunday

Frame B

Upper-dominant after the long-run day: back, shoulders, chest and arms without creating lower-body debt.

Phase 01

Establish. Repeat. Build. Hold. Absorb. Close.

The six-week phase is not a straight volume climb. Weeks 1–4 establish and develop the work, Week 5 pulls volume back, and Week 6 closes with comparable strength and physique evidence rather than a max-out test.

The load authoring gate is closed for Week 1 calibration: start from the working loads Tinius supplied, record actual load/reps/RIR, and progress only when the rep-range rule is met. The program is built around the equipment he actually owns, not a pretend gym.

Evidence

Keep the systems separate enough to learn from them.

FORM records the running. FORGE records the strength. The study watches the interaction: strength progress, physique change, running response, fatigue, and whether either system begins stealing from the other.

Current rule: no fourth strength day, no bonus sets, and no random exercise additions because a session felt easy.

FORM · OBSERVATION-LED PROGRESSION · OCTOBER 6

Judge what the whole week makes possible.

For Tinius, useful strength must coexist with useful running. We track pacing, rhythm, leg effort and recovery across both. More exhaustion or bonus sets are not the objective. The current running progression and strength limits remain in place.

The shared practice is to observe, choose, repeat and review. It is not a claim that one workout works for everyone. How FORM approaches the work.

Weekly priority

Thursday is earned.

Tuesday owns the primary running question. Thursday strength serves the week only when Tuesday is absorbed; a full FORM Thursday workout and a full lower-body Forge session are not automatically stacked. Missed support is not debt.
