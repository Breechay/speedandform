Theory

Short success is not the same as race durability.

Simon can look good for shorter work. That may not be his main limiter. His later specific work grows the distance he can hold at the selected pace. The pace does not need to get faster every week.

Elijah does not have seven months. His job is simpler: find the fastest pace he can carry into Savannah without spending the race in training.
