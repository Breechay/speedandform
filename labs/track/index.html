Go run something.

01 · The planHow Track is meant to work.

One room.
Different relationships.

Track is free. A plan decides when you run. Coaching decides what comes next. The room itself does not change.

This is the intended shape, written down so it can be argued with. None of it is shipped. What exists today is the authored work further down this page.

Everyone will enter the same room. What changes is how much of it is decided for you.

00 · ArrivalNew hereRead the standards without an account. That part is true today.

The rule that matters: a session you ran as a free athlete must not disappear when you buy a plan or hire a coach. The relationship gets richer; the record does not restart.

02 · StandardsThe target stays fixed.

Six standards.
Fixed targets.

A standard is a named session with set targets that do not move. That is what makes a mark comparable to your last one, and to everybody else’s.

Read a sheet like this: reps × distance, the recovery that belongs to that set, the target time for a single rep, and that same target as a pace per mile and per kilometer so you can enter whichever one your watch uses. Score is hits out of the reps on the sheet. A standard is added by Brice, and not every good session becomes one.

03 · ThresholdHeld, not scored.

Threshold work
is held.

A standard is scored rep by rep. Threshold work is not. You either hold the pace for the whole block or you do not. The mark is the pace you held.

012 × 10 minTwenty minutes of work. The entry rung.20 min work
022 × 12 minThe same shape, four minutes longer.24 min work
032 × 15 minThirty minutes in two pieces.30 min work
0425 min continuousLess total work than the rung before it, run in one piece.25 min work
053 × 10 minWhere the ladder ends. Thirty minutes of work, three entries into the effort.30 min work

The ladder is not strictly increasing in total work. Twenty-five minutes continuous sits above 2 × 15 in the order, and 3 × 10 closes it.

04 · Time trialsOne number each.

Time trials
are recorded.

No score, no hold. A trial produces one number and a date, and that number is the reference everything else is read against.

011 MileThe sharpest reference. Short enough to be honest about speed.1,609 m
022 MileLong enough that how you pace it decides the result.3,219 m
035KThe most portable reference. Easiest to repeat under the same conditions.5,000 m
0410KA longer reference. Conditions matter more, so record them.10,000 m
05Yasso 800sA repeated 800 protocol. Read it as a session you can compare, not as a marathon prediction.800 m reps

A trial is only a reference while it is current. Keep the date and how you ran it. A 5K from last spring and a 5K from last week are not the same evidence, and no fixed offset converts one distance into another.

05 · MarksThree kinds of record.

How a mark
reads.

Three kinds of work, three ways of reading it. Whether you write it on paper today or the app keeps it later, a mark means the same thing.

8 / 9

A standard is scored

Hits out of total reps. Nine reps in Pyramid Intervals, so nine chances to land inside the target. A missed rep is a miss, not a rep you drop.

6:17

A threshold is held

The pace you actually held for the whole block, with the block it belonged to. There is no partial credit and no score out of anything.

4:58

A trial is recorded

One time, one date. The conditions belong to the record too, because they decide whether it can be compared to the next one.

A full set of hits establishes the standard. It does not retire it. An established mark is the thing the next attempt is measured against. And an attempt carries where it came from: run on its own, or run as part of a plan or an assigned week. That is why a standalone 12 / 12 must never be quietly presented as plan work.

Your marks are yours. Track is not a leaderboard and will not rank athletes against each other. A standard is fixed so your attempts stay comparable to each other over time.

06 · CoachingTrack, then Plan, then Coaching.

Track is useful
on its own.

A plan adds authored sequence. Coaching gives Brice authority over your development. Each step is worth something without the next one.

01 · Product ladder

Track → Plan → Coaching.

Track is free and complete as a room. A plan provides authored sequence: what to run, in what order, on which day. Coaching is the step where someone takes responsibility for the direction.

02 · Universal room

Everyone can enter.

Free athletes run sessions. Plan athletes see the same canonical sessions connect to their week automatically. Coached athletes browse the whole room and see their marks without gaining a back door to freelance their prescription.

03 · Continuity

Nothing resets upward.

Attempts you made as a free athlete remain when you buy a plan or work with Brice. The relationship gets richer; the athlete record does not restart.

04 · Advisor position

Manage development, not workouts.

Coaching is about decisions over time: choosing the right problem, deciding what comes next, and saying what the evidence means. The workouts are only part of it.
