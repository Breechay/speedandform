Opening journal entry

The page begins where the evidence actually is, not where the plan hopes to end.

23 SEP 2026
Before Week 01
Coach record
Simon's last durability block moved the half from roughly 1:31 to 1:26 in three months. He fades late, not early. He can handle easy running and distance. His 17:35 5K says there is speed above the half. The next question is whether we can create more room above the pace while teaching the pace to last longer.
What is now known

The previous durability block worked.

  • The half moved by roughly five minutes across the prior three-month block.
  • The next half-marathon performance envelope is 1:19–1:23, with approximately 1:20 as the central working question.
  • Simon trains in kilometers. The study and athlete-facing prescription stay metric-native.
What still needs attention

The ceiling number is not settled.

  • Recent coach impression: threshold near 6:00/mi.
  • Previous training data: approximately 6:12–6:20/mi.
  • That difference is large enough to change the prescription. Week 1 resolves it instead of pretending it does not exist.

The question

One study question. Two things are allowed to move.

The study does not ask whether Simon is tough enough.

It asks what happens when two adaptations are developed at the same time.
Can the ceiling rise while race-pace duration also rises, without either curve undermining the other?

03

The method

The plan name is descriptive. The study name is editorial. The Two Curves is the study; Ceiling × Durability is the training method underneath it.

Tuesday

Race-pace duration

Make approximately 1:20 half-marathon pace increasingly ordinary. Progress the amount and continuity before chasing a faster number.

Broken → longer pieces → shorter reset → first continuous ask.
Thursday

Ceiling

Develop the high-aerobic ceiling as its own instrument. The starting reference comes from current 5K evidence, not a fixed subtraction from goal pace.

Establish → expose → extend → retest → recalibrate.
Saturday

Durability context

Keep the endurance chassis underneath the experiment. Early long runs stay deliberately untested so Tuesday and Thursday remain legible.

Time on the legs without stealing the answer from the key sessions.
Week 01

Establish the instruments

What does the future race pace cost right now? Where does controlled threshold actually sit?

Weeks 02–04

Both curves rise

Tuesday adds race-pace continuity while Thursday develops high-aerobic exposure. Neither progression is allowed to erase the other.

Week 05

Gate 01

Eight kilometers continuous at working half pace and a separate 5K ceiling read. Block 02 is written from those results, not in advance.

Scope

The page is allowed to be unfinished because the athlete is still answering it.

What this page can say

Simon improved materially during the prior durability block. The new block deliberately develops race-pace duration and the ceiling above it in parallel. The first five weeks are authored and the Week 5 gate determines what comes next.

What this page does not claim

It does not claim that VO₂max has increased, that 1:20 is already available for a half marathon, that the unresolved threshold number is known, or that the May race date is fixed. Those become facts only when the evidence supports them.
