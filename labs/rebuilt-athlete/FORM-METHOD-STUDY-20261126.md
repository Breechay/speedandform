# Study 002 · The athlete, the method, the game
26 November 2026 · Research framing

The injury is the starting condition, not the permanent identity of the study. The subject is now training in FORM to develop speed, threshold, endurance and muscular strength together. The question is whether the method and its software help him make better training decisions and absorb increasingly demanding work. The app is an intervention to evaluate, not an outcome that validates itself.

## Three distinct layers
- Physical development: pace exposure, longest sustained work, matched-pace HR and effort, mechanics, actual strength sets and loads, and recovery.
- Training decisions: respect sequence, easy-running speed ceiling, assigned threshold band and recovery format, long-run dose, strength placement, and deliberate changes when recovery calls for them.
- Product behavior: did the athlete see and use the game's feedback, did it reflect the intended work correctly, and did it change a subsequent decision? Completion, a brick or a level alone is not evidence of adaptation or behavior change.

FORM's governing sequence is stimulate, absorb, progress. Preserve that sequence; no make-up mileage or extra effort to win points. Record exceptions rather than erasing them. A deliberate recovery change can be sound even when a planned session does not count.

## The current rules to test
Read current athlete-specific prescriptions and canonical game rules before interpreting an activity. As of November 26, FORM's actual easy-running guardrail for Brice is 480 seconds/mile: 8:00/mi or slower. This is a speed ceiling (not a target pace, HR ceiling or universal rule). FORM's aerobic tower rule says work over the ceiling is logged, not counted. Its threshold rule says work outside the band is logged, not counted. Its speed rule values quality reps and full rest; its endurance rule does not reward extra distance beyond the plan. Strength records and the applicable tower rules must be read together, with no inference of repetitions, loads, RIR or clearance from a set count.

These are the rule texts observed in FORM, not a test that every shipped client or scorer enforces them. Verify actual awards, exceptions and receipt matching before saying a rule fired. Skill (week), Mastery (demonstrated capability), Capacity (paired comparisons), Experience (history) are different constructs; assigned or assessed ability must not be mislabeled as demonstrated. The game should reinforce doing the right work, not maximize training stress.

Do not grant a threshold promotion from this evidence alone or assume how the scorer treated it.
- Running and strength assignments and completed strength receipts flow from the same FORM record. Compare actual lower-body load and the next hard run, not merely the planned schedule. Record units, repetitions and reserve only when available; duration-only records are separate.

## Weekly review
For each meaningful decision, record intended dose and sequence (with the applicable version and guardrail), what was actually filed, whether it matched, why a departure occurred if reported, what the app awarded or showed if observed, and the next-day/24–48-hour response if reported. Distinguish absent data from failure. In weekly review, report denominators and coverage: eligible easy receipts with readable pace / eligible receipts, in-band work repetitions / readable repetitions, assigned vs actual order and separation of hard running and lower strength, recovery changes, and observed game feedback vs expected rule. Do not use a single compliance percentage or require a perfect week. Compare changes over time, with matched conditions where feasible.

Questions: Does feedback help keep easy runs easy? Does it discourage making threshold a race? Does it protect the sequence and encourage appropriate recovery, rather than extra sessions? Does the athlete increasingly reproduce speed at lower cost? Does strength development coexist with running progress?

Publish only curated subject data, never private notes verbatim, GPS or other athletes' records. The study is a single-athlete observational case study; it can test plausibility, reveal defects and guide program design. It cannot establish that FORM caused a change or generalize efficacy to other athletes. Keep counterexamples and null observations; no automatic prescription changes or phase promotions. A recommendation returns to FORM for athlete/coach decision. No additional compulsory questionnaire.


## Prospective evaluation agreement · 26 November 2026

Before the next collection period, fix the questions and the conditions under which the study will report that a target was not met. The following are practical single-athlete evaluation targets, not established clinical cutoffs, universal programming standards or proof of causation. Record future amendments prospectively with date, reason and effective period; do not rewrite prior results. Start each prospective window on the first local Monday after the relevant feedback/receipt feature is deployed and its capture has passed acceptance tests. Report pre-rollout observations separately.

| Question | Window and denominator | Prespecified negative signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Easy-running restraint | 8 weeks; assigned easy runs actually filed with usable pace and the speed ceiling applicable to that assignment. Compare whole-run average to ceiling (8:00/mi at agreement); report sample-level violations separately where readable. | More than 20% of eligible runs are faster than the applicable ceiling. |
| Threshold restraint | 8 weeks; actually filed assigned threshold sessions with all prescribed work bouts readable and the assignment-time pace band available. A session is fully in band only if each required work bout is in band; document tolerance/rounding rules at start. Record recovery and completion separately. | Fewer than two thirds of eligible sessions land fully in the band. |
| Strength capacity | 12 weeks; same movement, equipment/load convention, comparable confirmed reps, recorded load and unit; track opportunities and executed work. | Strength Tower continues to fill but no comparable load best is observed. Treat this as a review flag, not proof that strength or the program failed. Repeatability, more reps at same load, stability and other gains may matter. |

Report the count and fraction of eligible, unreadable, excused, changed and unfiled assignments separately. Do not count missing receipts as violations or success; insufficient comparable observations produce an inconclusive result. A departure explicitly chosen for recovery is not automatically a poor decision. Keep data capture and UI feedback coverage separate from outcome eligibility. If rules or a prescription change, use the effective assignment and scorer inputs actually used at the relevant time, not the newest values. Do not substitute the game award for the independent study measure: the currently inspected form_true_easy implementation prioritizes heart-rate evidence when available, then pace-estimate/RPE fallbacks. Its verdict is not synonymous with adherence to the study's 8:00/mi speed ceiling. Investigate any disagreement rather than silently redefining either measure.

Baseline only: November 23 and 25 met the easy ceiling by whole-run average (2/2 readable); the November 26 threshold session did not meet its then-assigned 6:20–6:30/mi band (0/3 work laps in range). These observations predate the proposed event capture and do not establish what the athlete saw or how a game award was applied. A later 6:15–6:25 band cannot retroactively change the November 26 comparison.

## Preserve what FORM said at the time

Section 68 is the prospective game-evidence contract. At filing, persist the actual canonical scorer's per-receipt, per-lane decision and its exact relevant inputs, prescription version, effective guardrails, rule/build version and reason. Keep Skill credit, Tower progression and demonstrated strength bests separate. A receipt may feed multiple lanes or none. Capture at the authoritative decision boundary, after relevant receipt pieces and matching are finalized, not by recomputing old sessions nightly. A nightly repair pass may identify missing evidence or record a new explicitly labeled retrospective evaluation, never impersonate an original verdict. Corrections append revisions linked to prior records; retries must be idempotent.

The client separately reports when a specific decision's reveal or progress/dial change was actually rendered, with app build, originating surface, presentation mode and observed timestamp. Absence of an event means presentation unknown, not that nothing appeared. Rendering is not proof that the athlete noticed, understood or acted on it. Existing athlete comments can establish perceived influence; no compulsory new questionnaire. Evidence is private by default; the study publishes only approved aggregate or curated subject observations. Never fabricate historical verdicts or repair missing set units/reps by assumption.

The weekly review must present product correctness, behavioral outcomes, physiological development and evidence coverage independently. A successful product experience can coincide with no measurable physical gain in a week; adaptation can occur even when the product fails. Do not infer product causation from an association. The athlete and coach review the findings and choose any plan or game changes.


## Calibration and coach judgment · clarification, 26 November 2026

The athlete explicitly clarified that today's 3 × 8-minute session was a calibration: he tried to sit back and find a comfortable working effort rather than deliberately exceed the assigned band. Preserve the original 6:20–6:30/mi assignment, observed 6:18/6:12/6:17 laps, reported RPE 7 and subsequent 6:15–6:25 prescription separately. The coach reports that the session already received Threshold Tower credit (Base 1 of 2); the study has not independently verified the historical award or presentation event. Calibration credit is justified by the intended learning and even execution, not by retroactively applying the new band. No claim that physiological threshold has been established by one session.

Proposed prospective scoring contract: distinguish ordinary prescribed threshold work from deliberately assigned calibration work. For ordinary sessions, use the filed prescription version and explicit, published execution criteria; evaluate readable required bouts and keep partial/unreadable evidence separate. Calibration needs its own declared purpose, effort/quality and completion criteria, ideally authored before execution. A coach may make an explicit exception after the session with a specific reason, author, timestamp and evidence; keep the unmodified numerical comparison and automated verdict alongside the coach award. No retroactive relabeling to make a missed pace target look successful. Preserve already-earned credit; do not rescore old sessions. Review calibration/coach exceptions independently from ordinary band-adherence outcomes; report all-session raw pace comparisons and exception counts so exceptions cannot hide unfavorable evidence. Game credit, demonstrated capacity and future prescription remain distinct. Global scoring rollout requires explicit approval and acceptance testing; the study clarification changes no app state.
