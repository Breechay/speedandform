# Strength and speed coordination (Study 002)

The 12-week speed-first running season (October 26, 2026–January 17, 2027) is documented in [Speed First. Distance Earned.](SPEED-FIRST-12-WEEK-20261022.md). The strength-and-speed field guide retains its October 19–25 dated recovery and strength sessions; future strength week changes require a separate coach decision. Preserve four possible strength slots as a planning reference: upper body two sessions with shoulder/rear-delt/upper-back emphasis, and two lower-body or accessory exposures with posterior-chain emphasis, placed so they do not interfere with the week’s high-speed track workout, easy aerobic support, and recovery. Do not infer that adding four heavy strength sessions is required or appropriate. The athlete’s existing Forge program remains the strength source and must not be silently overwritten by this study design.
