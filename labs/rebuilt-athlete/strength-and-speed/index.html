02 / CHOICE ARCHITECTURE

One purpose per session.

No chasing all objectives every day. Choose A for upper strength, B for lower strength, C for a short physique-focused accessory session, and D for elastic preparation. A, B, and C are complete voluntary strength options, not mandatory weekly appointments. Elastic work is preserved for a later phase and is not part of the current workout bank.

Start resistance work with five to ten minutes of general preparation and unloaded/reduced-load practice sets. Working loads should allow about two to three good reps in reserve; avoid grinders and maximal efforts. Keep running and lifting recovery costs visible together.

    09 / STUDY MEASUREMENT

    What proves the work matters?

    Training doesn't become productive by generating more numbers. It becomes measurable when the numbers answer your actual question: does faster running become available naturally, stay available longer, and cost less?

    Running exposureDuration

    15-second pace ranges; repeat duration; continuous distance; exactly identified source.

    Aerobic responseHR + RPE

    Compare pace with HR and perceived effort in matched conditions. VO₂ is an estimated supporting trend.

    MovementGCT + stride

    Compare at similar pace and context. Shorter contact/longer stride alone isn't a score.

    DurabilityAfterward

    Next-day tibia response, global fatigue, sleep, consistency, time outside and satisfaction.
