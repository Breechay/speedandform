04 · Between blocks

TWO WEEKS.
NO TEST.

First recover. Then return to normal running. Being tired after a race is not missing work, and nothing in these two weeks needs to prove fitness.
Week 01 · Dec 06–12

RECOVER

Unload Orlando and the 15-week accumulation. Absence of intensity is intentional, not missing work.

  • 4–5 running days maximum
  • ~24–30 miles or less if response asks for less
  • Easy only · no pace requirement
  • Race debrief captured
  • No long-run achievement to prove
Week 02 · Dec 13–19

RETURN

Restore rhythm without creating a new performance question.

  • 5–6 running days
  • ~34–40 miles
  • Easy frequency returns
  • Short strides or hills if comfortable
  • Relaxed 9–10 mile long run · no test

06 · Four hypotheses

WHAT WE THINK.
BEFORE WE KNOW.

These are claims the work can weaken. Athlete branching and fueling rules are part of the study method; they are not hypotheses pretending to be outcomes.
01Can the pace still show up late?
02Can faster work stay available?
03Does patience early matter?
04Does the smaller task stop being too expensive?
H01 · Late access

CAN IT STILL SHOW UP LATE?

If Act I establishes the reserve continuously, progressively increasing the running before it should preserve or improve access under accumulated load.

H02 · Ceiling preservation

CAN FASTER WORK STAY AVAILABLE?

Controlled threshold and aerobic-power performance should remain broadly stable through the marathon block without repeatedly degrading Saturday availability.

H03 · Restraint

DOES PATIENCE EARLY MATTER?

What the athlete spends before the decisive segment changes what remains available when the marathon becomes expensive.

H04 · Cost / absorption

DOES THE SMALLER TASK SETTLE?

After the principal Closing-10K exposure, a deliberately smaller late-reserve task should be completed without disproportionate cost or prolonged recovery.

07 · What would change our mind?

WHAT WOULD
PROVE US WRONG?

No single watch number gets to decide the story. We compare the run, the athlete's report, fueling, conditions and recovery — then decide what the evidence actually supports.
H01 weakens ifLater placement repeatedly removes access.

The reserve becomes unavailable despite controlled openings, deliberate fueling and otherwise comparable conditions.

H02 weakens ifFaster performance deteriorates or repeatedly steals from Saturday.

Comparable threshold/aerobic-power work becomes materially harder or slower, or leaves enough residual fatigue to repeatedly degrade the governing Saturday question.

H03 weakens ifOpening cost shows no useful relationship to what remains late.

Across comparable work, late availability varies independently of how conservatively the prior running was distributed.

H04 weakens ifThe reduced task still carries disproportionate cost.

The smaller W7 ask requires unusually high effort, the reserve disappears, mechanics materially deteriorate, or recovery remains meaningfully impaired.

What we locked before OrlandoTHE QUESTIONS STAY PUT. THE ANSWERS CAN CHANGE.

This is a two-athlete coaching case study, not a controlled trial. We can show what happened to Hope and José and whether the idea survived contact with them. We cannot pretend two runners prove a universal rule.

FuelingFrom W4, fuel is prescribed.

Use the race-intent strategy and record product, amount, timing and tolerance. A late fade under improvised fueling does not cleanly answer the durability question.

Orlando decision frameworkOutput · cost · distribution · context · recovery · athlete report.

Each athlete is reassigned independently. The next step can keep, redefine, protect/delay, or branch the reserve/rhythm assignment. No single noisy metric decides the verdict.

Recovery protectionAbnormal recovery can stop the next Ask.

Two materially abnormal recovery reports trigger review before the next major Ask. The response can reduce, replace, delay, or proceed only once normal easy-running function returns.

AmendmentsOriginal claims stay visible.

Any post-Orlando change keeps the original prescription, date, triggering evidence and revised read. FORM does not silently rewrite what it believed before the outcome.
