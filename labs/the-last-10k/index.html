06 · Four hypotheses

WHAT WE THINK.
BEFORE WE KNOW.

These are claims the work can weaken. Athlete branching and fueling rules are part of the study method; they are not hypotheses pretending to be outcomes.
H01 · Late access

Move the capability later.

If Act I establishes the reserve continuously, progressively increasing the running before it should preserve or improve access under accumulated load.

H02 · Ceiling preservation

Do not trade speed for distance.

Keeping controlled faster work in the season should preserve the relationship between the reserve and what sits above it without compromising Saturday.

H03 · Restraint

The closing 10K starts before mile 20.

What the athlete spends before the decisive segment changes what remains available when the marathon becomes expensive.

H04 · Cost

Progress is not always a bigger ask.

After the principal closing-10K exposure, a smaller late reserve task should be repeatable with lower visible and reported cost.

07 · What would change our mind?

THE STUDY HAS TO BE
ALLOWED TO LOSE.

No one number decides the verdict. We compare execution, athlete report, relevant physiological signals, mechanics where actually observed, fueling, conditions and 24–72 hour recovery.
H01 weakens ifLater placement repeatedly removes access.

The reserve becomes unavailable despite controlled openings, deliberate fueling and otherwise comparable conditions.

H02 weakens ifCeiling work reliably steals from the marathon question.

Faster sessions repeatedly reduce Saturday quality or recovery enough that preserving the ceiling is costing more than it contributes.

H03 weakens ifOpening cost shows no useful relationship to what remains late.

Across comparable work, late availability varies independently of how conservatively the prior running was distributed.

H04 weakens ifThe smaller confirmation is not cheaper.

W7 costs the same or more than the larger W6 exposure under comparable fueling and conditions.

Fueling ruleFrom W4, fuel is prescribed.

Use the race-intent strategy, then record amount, timing, product and tolerance. A late fade under improvised fueling does not answer the durability question cleanly.

Branching ruleOne theory can become two assignments.

Hope and José do not stay symmetrical merely because they started the study together. Orlando and later evidence can branch the resolved work.
