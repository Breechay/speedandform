07

Useful questions.

Before you show up
Do I need to be fast to join Thursday?

No. Thursday uses short distances with full recovery, and no specific pace is required to arrive. Run your own effort. The session is shared; the pace is not.

Is FORM a run club?

Thursday is a community running session with a coached structure. You can absolutely come because you want people to run with. FORM also goes further than a social meetup: individual coaching can organize the rest of your week around your goals.

How do I find the monthly long run?

Follow @form.practice for the next confirmed route. Email run announcements will also be available here when the signup is live.

Can Brice coach me for a race?

Yes. Run Development is individual coaching, so race preparation can be built from your current training rather than from a generic finish-time target.
