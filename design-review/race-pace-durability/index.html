WHY THIS BLOCK EXISTS

Speed isn’t the problem.
Carrying it is.

The previous block developed threshold capacity. This block shifts the emphasis toward race-pace durability: progressively extending how far 6:30–6:45/mi can be carried continuously while threshold work remains in the program to maintain the ceiling.
