What to do when the week goes wrong

You will miss days. What matters is what you do with the days that remain.

Every real training life includes broken weeks — work runs long, kids get sick, sleep falls apart. A missed session is not a failed plan. The failure is what most people do next: try to cram the missed work back in.

Do not stack the hard days

The week has a shape for a reason. Hard days are spaced so you can actually perform them, and the sessions between are doing quiet work. When you compress two hard days together to catch up, you usually get one bad session and one dangerous one.

Skip forward instead. Rejoin the week where it actually is, not where you wish you were.

One honest week beats a perfect plan

A week where you did three of five sessions well is a good week. A week where you forced all five, half-recovered and resentful, is how plans die. Consistency is measured in months, and months forgive individual days completely.

Let the app hold the order

This is exactly the job FORGE and FORM were built for. FORGE opens sessions in sequence, so a missed day does not scramble the program — the order waits for you. FORM keeps the week's relationships intact, so what belongs today is always clear, even after a broken stretch. You do not renegotiate the plan. You open the app and do what is there.

Miss the day, keep the week. Open the app tomorrow and do what belongs.
