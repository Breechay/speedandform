A goal is not a training pace.

The pace chart does the arithmetic. Your recent running tells you what you can repeat now.

What does the evidence say?

The week keeps falling apart.

Look at frequency, recovery and what fits your life. Build a repeatable week before adding a demanding session.

You start fast and fade.

Compare opening pace, effort, conditions and fueling with your preparation. First consider a more realistic start.

The start feels controlled, then the race slips away.

Review the whole block and race. Training, pacing, food, drink and conditions can all matter. A fade does not diagnose one missing quality.

You race evenly and want to move faster.

Review recent race and workout evidence. Choose a block with a clear purpose while keeping easy running manageable.

Workouts improve, but races do not.

Ask whether the session is serving the race, how much it costs and whether you recover well enough to use it.

Three different numbers.

Goal pace is the arithmetic for a finish time. A current race estimate comes from recent evidence. Training pace serves a particular session. They are not interchangeable.

Use the pace chart to understand a target, not to prescribe every run from it.

Give each session a job.

Know what you are practicing: controlled faster running, rhythm at a race-relevant effort, or time on your feet. Finish with enough reserve to recover for the next useful run.

Broken work and continuous work ask different things. Completing repetitions does not automatically prove you can hold the same pace continuously. Neither format is always the better next step.

Do not automatically increase distance and speed together each week. Progress when execution and recovery support the change.

Write down enough to decide.

Record what you did, the conditions, whether you could control the effort and how you felt the next day. Look for a pattern across weeks, not a verdict from one hard session.

If symptoms are limiting normal training, address that before adding faster work. A generic guide cannot choose an individual return plan.
