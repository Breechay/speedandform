Why this exists

There is plenty of running content. Sequence is harder to find.

This course collects serious lectures, conference talks and expert teaching on the systems underneath endurance performance. It starts with training principles, then moves through physiology, biomechanics, tissue adaptation, strength and programming.

The point is not to collect terminology. It is to understand enough mechanism to make better decisions about training, coaching and the evidence in front of you.

How to study it

Do not turn it into homework.

01 · Watch

Start in order. Give each lecture enough time to establish its question, then keep moving.

02 · Write

What did I learn? What changed how I think? Where does this show up in my own running or coaching?

03 · Test

Bring the idea back to actual training. A mechanism becomes useful when it helps you observe, prescribe or interpret more clearly.

Prestige does not earn completion. A lecture still has to teach.

If a video is repetitive, unclear or simply does not help you learn, move on. The course is curated to reduce noise, not create another completion badge.

What this is / what it is not

A learning path, not a certification.

This is a public curation of material I use to deepen my own coaching and understanding. It is not individualized training, medical advice, a substitute for formal education, or an endorsement of every claim made in every lecture.

Good science still has context. Study populations, measurement methods and uncertainty matter. When a broad norm conflicts with the athlete in front of you, inspect the evidence before forcing the athlete to fit the average.
