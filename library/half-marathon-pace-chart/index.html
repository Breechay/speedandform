Chart coverage is not an event cutoff or an eligibility test. Use the split calculator for other times and distances.

Why the rounded pace does not multiply back exactly.

A 1:30:00 half marathon displays as 6:52 per mile or 4:16 per kilometer. Running exactly either displayed pace gives a finish just over 1:30:00, because the displayed pace has been rounded.

The calculator keeps the original precision for each split. Do not turn display rounding into a blanket instruction to run faster.

Use the number in context.

Plan your opening from your actual preparation and the conditions. Keep any walk breaks practiced. If a split goes astray, reassess the effort before trying to regain time.

A goal time is not a current easy pace or threshold pace. Those need evidence from your training, not a conversion from your ambition.
