Form cues.

  • Long neckKeep your chin level. Look at the ground a little way ahead.
  • Quiet shouldersKeep your shoulders low and still. Only your arms swing.
  • Four lightsKeep your shoulders right on top of your hips.
  • Elbows backPush your elbows back. Let them swing forward on their own.
  • Low handsKeep your hands low, close to your hips.
  • Soft handsKeep your hands loose, never in a fist.
  • Hips forwardRun tall with your hips forward, right under your shoulders.
  • Tall springRun tall, with soft knees.
  • Land under youLet each foot land right under your hips, not out in front.
  • Quick feetTake light, quick steps. Your feet leave the ground fast.
  • Straight feetPoint your toes straight ahead when each foot lands.
