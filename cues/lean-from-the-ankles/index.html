Lean from the ankles

Tip your whole body forward a little, from the ankles, not the waist.

Picture it

Pretend you are a tall tree starting to tip over. Your legs run to keep up.

Try it now

Stand tall, feet together. Lean forward until you have to step. Run 50 meters holding that angle.

You feel pulled forward, not pushed.

Why it works

A small lean lets your body weight pull you forward, so your legs push less for the same speed.

On easy runs, think about it for 30 seconds, three times. In hard workouts, use it on the last rep. It is yours when it still holds in the last ten minutes of your long run.
