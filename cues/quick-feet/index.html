Quick feet

Take light, quick steps. Your feet leave the ground fast.

Picture it

Pretend the ground is hot. Touch it and get your foot off fast.

Try it now

Count your right foot for 30 seconds. Then add two or three more steps at the same speed.

Lighter, quieter steps, as if you are barely touching the ground.

Why it works

Quick, light steps spend less time braking and put less pounding on your legs.

On easy runs, think about it for 30 seconds, three times. In hard workouts, use it on the last rep. It is yours when it still holds in the last ten minutes of your longest run of the week.
