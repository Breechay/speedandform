Low hands

Keep your hands low, close to your hips.

Picture it

Reach for your shorts pockets every time your arm swings back.

Try it now

Bend your elbows to about a right angle and swing so your thumbs brush the side of your shorts.

Your shoulders sink and your arms feel lighter.

Why it works

High hands make your shoulders tight. Low, loose hands let your arms help your legs.

On easy runs, think about it for 30 seconds, three times. Then run normally and notice the difference. In hard workouts, use it on the last rep.
