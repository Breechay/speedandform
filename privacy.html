Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

FORM is a running training app. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is stored, and your rights as a user. Your training data is stored on your device and, when you connect to FORM coaching, in our coaching service. The app does not use advertising tracking. Website inquiry handling and advertising measurement are described below.

FORM coaching is optional. If you connect it, the app sends your coaching account identifier, assigned plan, filed session details, effort rating, athlete words, and any symptom you choose to record to the Speed and Form coaching service so your assigned coach can provide the service. FORM does not sell or share this information with third parties.

01

Information We Collect

FORM collects only the information necessary to provide the app experience. This includes:

Account information. When you sign in with Apple, Apple provides FORM with a unique user identifier and, if you choose, your name. FORM stores this identifier locally on your device to maintain your session. We do not receive or store your Apple ID email address unless you explicitly choose to share it.

Training data. Your training log entries, session history, Ghost Protocol progress, cycle records, paces, skip reasons, and onboarding preferences are stored on your device using Apple's native storage systems. If you are signed into iCloud on your device, this data also syncs to your personal iCloud account so it is available across your devices and persists if you reinstall the app. See Section 03 for more detail.

Optional coaching data. If you connect to FORM coaching, we process your account identifier, assigned training plan, session completions, distance and duration, RPE, athlete words, and symptoms you deliberately file. This information is used only to deliver coaching, is visible only to the athlete and their assigned coaching team, and is retained until you request deletion.

Notification preferences. If you grant permission, FORM schedules local notifications on your device (session reminders). These are generated and delivered entirely on-device. No notification data is sent to any server.

Diagnostic data. FORM uses Apple's MetricKit framework to receive crash and performance diagnostic reports. This data is written to your device's local Documents folder only. MetricKit data is provided by Apple and governed by Apple's own privacy practices.

02

Information We Do Not Collect

The FORM app does not collect, access, or transmit the following:

We do not access Apple Health or HealthKit data. We do not use third-party analytics services (such as Firebase, Mixpanel, or similar). We do not use advertising networks or tracking identifiers. We do not collect location data. We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library. We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

03

How Your Data Is Stored

FORM stores training information on your device and, depending on features you choose, in iCloud and the FORM coaching service:

On your device. All data is saved to your device's local storage using Apple's native systems. This data is available immediately and works without an internet connection.

In your iCloud account. If you are signed into iCloud on your device, FORM also writes your training data to iCloud Key-Value Storage — a feature of Apple's iCloud platform that syncs data across your own devices and preserves it if you reinstall the app. This data lives in your personal iCloud account and is not accessible to Speed and Form. It is governed by Apple's iCloud privacy policy.

In the FORM coaching service. Connected coaching data is stored in a restricted service so your assigned coach can see the plan and the sessions you file. The service is not used for advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking.

You can manage or disable iCloud sync at any time through your device's Settings → Apple ID → iCloud. Disabling iCloud for FORM will stop syncing but will not delete data already stored on your device.

04

Sign in with Apple

FORM uses Sign in with Apple as its authentication method. Apple's privacy-preserving login system means Apple authenticates your identity — FORM does not receive your Apple ID credentials or password.

You may choose to hide your email from FORM when signing in. If you do, Apple provides a private relay address that is not stored or used by FORM.

05

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your FORM account at any time from within the app. Deleting your account permanently removes all locally stored data and revokes your Sign in with Apple credential for FORM. If your account is connected to FORM coaching, the app also sends a deletion request and immediately revokes coaching access. We remove or irreversibly anonymize connected server data within 30 days. This action cannot be undone.

To also remove data stored in iCloud, go to Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage and remove FORM's stored data from there.

06

Children's Privacy

FORM is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

07

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of FORM after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. For material changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify users through the app.

08

Third-Party Services

FORM integrates only with Apple's native frameworks: Sign in with Apple, iCloud Key-Value Storage, UserNotifications, and MetricKit. Each of these is governed by Apple's own privacy policy, available at apple.com/legal/privacy.

09

Website inquiries and measurement

When you inquire about coaching, analysis, photography or AI setup at speedandform.com, we use the contact details and information you provide to respond and discuss the work. New commercial inquiries are stored in Brice’s private operating console using Supabase. They do not create an athlete account, book a session, take payment or subscribe you to marketing email. Some older contact forms use FormSubmit for email delivery.

The form includes the selected offer, the page you used and advertising source tags when available. A random submission identifier helps avoid duplicate inquiries on retry. The browser keeps that identifier and source tags in session storage; contact details are not saved there. Please do not include medical records, passwords or other sensitive information.

Public commercial pages use Google Analytics and Meta Pixel to measure page visits and accepted inquiry submissions. These services may receive the page URL, IP address, browser information and cookie identifiers. Our event code does not send your intake answers, name or email address to these services. App training records are not used for website advertising measurement.

The half-marathon Library resources use Google Analytics to measure page visits, deliberate week opens, unit changes, print requests and clicks to related resources. These events contain the resource and version, fixed action labels and public campaign tags, not calculator inputs or private training information. Public campaign tags can stay in session storage and follow links to related public pages. Google Signals and advertising personalization are disabled for this resource measurement. The resources do not load Meta Pixel.

We do not load these measurement tools when your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. You can also block advertising trackers in your browser. Learn about Meta's processing and controls in Meta's privacy policy. Contact brice@speedandform.com to request deletion of your coaching inquiry.
