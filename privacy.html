Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Speed & Form: Running (also called FORM) is a running and strength training app. This policy explains what the app collects, where it is kept, and your choices. Some of your training stays on your phone; what belongs to your FORM account (your plan, the sessions you file, your messages and anything you buy) is kept in the FORM service so it follows you to any phone. FORM does not use advertising tracking and does not sell your information. Website inquiries and advertising measurement are described in section 09.

If you are coached, the plan your coach writes and the sessions, efforts, notes and any symptom you choose to record are shared with your coach so they can coach you. If you buy a program in the app, Apple processes the payment; FORM receives a record of the purchase so it can put the program in your Week. Nothing you file is used for advertising.

01

Information We Collect

FORM collects what it needs to run the app and the service behind it:

Account information. You can sign in with Apple, with Google, or with a code sent to your email. We receive an account identifier, your email address (or Apple’s private relay address if you hide it) and, if you give it, your name. A portrait is stored only if you add one.

Training you file. The sessions you file (distance, duration, paces, reps, effort, how it felt, your words, and any symptom you choose to record), your plan, your program days and your strength sessions are stored in your FORM account. Some working data (drafts, preferences, recent views) stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account.

Coaching. If you are coached, your coach sees your plan, what you file, your messages and the voice notes they leave you. If you ask about coaching or an evaluation in the app, we keep your request, your note and your contact details so Brice can reply.

Purchases. Purchases in the app are made through Apple. FORM receives Apple’s signed record of the purchase (the product, transaction identifiers, date and whether it was a test purchase) and links it to your account. Apple tells us if a purchase is refunded or revoked. We never see your card or payment details.

Group runs and FORM Thursday. If you join group runs, we keep your name, which runs you said you’re going to, and photos you choose to share with the group.

Apple Health. If you allow it, FORM reads running workouts and recovery signals (sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability) from Apple Health to show your runs and your recovery. FORM never writes to Apple Health, and Health data is never used for advertising or marketing.

Strava. If you connect Strava, FORM reads your activities from Strava so you can file them. You can disconnect at any time.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, FORM schedules reminders on your device (for example, to file a session) and we keep a device token so the service can tell you when your coach writes to you or a run you’re in changes.

How the app is used. FORM records which screens are opened and which actions are taken (for example, that a session was recorded or a program was viewed) to understand what works and fix what doesn’t. Signed-in events are linked to your account; events from someone not signed in carry only a random identifier made on that phone. This stays in FORM’s own service and is never shared with an analytics or advertising company.

Diagnostics. Apple’s MetricKit gives FORM crash and performance reports on your device.

02

What We Don’t Do

We don’t use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the app, and we don’t track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. We don’t collect your location. We don’t read your contacts. The camera is used only when you take a portrait or progress photo, the microphone only when a coach records a voice note, and the photo library only to save an image you choose or to pick one you choose. FORM can add a run you’re going to your calendar; it never reads your calendar.

03

Where Your Data Is Kept

On your device. Drafts, preferences and recent views are saved with Apple’s storage on your phone so the app works without a connection.

In your iCloud account. If you use iCloud, some of that device data syncs through Apple’s iCloud Key-Value Storage to your own devices. Speed and Form can’t read it. You can turn this off in Settings → Apple ID → iCloud.

In the FORM service. Your account, what you file, your plan and program, your messages, purchase records and usage events are kept in FORM’s service, hosted by Supabase. Access is limited to you and, for coached athletes, your coach. It is not used for advertising.

04

Signing In

With Sign in with Apple or Google, they confirm who you are; FORM never receives your password. You can hide your email from FORM with Apple; FORM then uses Apple’s relay address. With an email code, the code is sent to your address by an email delivery service.

05

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your FORM account at any time from within the app. Deleting your account permanently removes all locally stored data and revokes your Sign in with Apple credential for FORM. If you have a FORM account, the app also sends a deletion request and immediately revokes coaching access. We remove or irreversibly anonymize your server data within 30 days. A record of an App Store purchase (the product, transaction identifiers and date) may be kept where it is needed for refunds, accounting or the law. Programs you bought stay with your Apple ID: sign in again and use Restore purchase. Deleting cannot be undone.

To also remove data stored in iCloud, go to Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage and remove FORM's stored data from there.

06

Children's Privacy

FORM is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

07

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of FORM after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. For material changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify users through the app.

08

Services We Use

Apple (Sign in with Apple, the App Store and in-app purchases, iCloud Key-Value Storage, notifications, Apple Health and MetricKit), Google (if you sign in with Google), Supabase (which hosts the FORM service), Strava (if you connect it) and an email delivery service (for sign-in codes). Each handles information under its own policy; Apple’s is at apple.com/legal/privacy.

09

Website inquiries and measurement

When you inquire about coaching, analysis, photography or AI setup at speedandform.com, we use the contact details and information you provide to respond and discuss the work. New commercial inquiries are stored in Brice’s private operating console using Supabase. They do not create an athlete account, book a session, take payment or subscribe you to marketing email. Some older contact forms use FormSubmit for email delivery.

The form includes the selected offer, the page you used and advertising source tags when available. A random submission identifier helps avoid duplicate inquiries on retry. The browser keeps that identifier and source tags in session storage; contact details are not saved there. Please do not include medical records, passwords or other sensitive information.

Public commercial pages use Google Analytics and Meta Pixel to measure page visits and accepted inquiry submissions. These services may receive the page URL, IP address, browser information and cookie identifiers. Our event code does not send your intake answers, name or email address to these services. App training records are not used for website advertising measurement.

The half-marathon Library resources use Google Analytics to measure page visits, deliberate week opens, unit changes, print requests and clicks to related resources. These events contain the resource and version, fixed action labels and public campaign tags, not calculator inputs or private training information. Public campaign tags can stay in session storage and follow links to related public pages. Google Signals and advertising personalization are disabled for this resource measurement. The resources do not load Meta Pixel.

We do not load these measurement tools when your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. You can also block advertising trackers in your browser. Learn about Meta's processing and controls in Meta's privacy policy. Contact brice@speedandform.com to request deletion of your coaching inquiry.
