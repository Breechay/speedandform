A few practical questions.

Know what you are choosing before you commit.

What is outside the $900 pilot?

A new custom application, a large data migration, complex CRM replacement, ongoing operations and regulated or sensitive-data workflows are outside this pilot. We can discuss a separate scope if needed.

Will it send messages automatically?

The default is a draft for you to review. Any automatic sending requires a separately agreed rule and approval during setup.

Do I need new software?

We start with what you already use. Any recommended subscriptions and their ongoing costs are identified before you commit. They are not included in the $900 fee.

Can you maintain it afterward?

We can discuss a small maintenance arrangement after the pilot, once the work and its support needs are known. There is no automatic subscription.
