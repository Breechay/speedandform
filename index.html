02 / Coaching

Hands on when it matters.

When something stands out, it has the quality of an out-of-tune note. I try one useful change, then watch the next rep.

That is gait retraining in practice: small changes to posture, rhythm or mechanics, repeated until they become part of the way you run.

Not everything needs fixing.Some things just cost you more.

Pace and heart rate tell me what happened. How the work felt, what you chose when it got hard, and whether the cue held help decide what comes next.

Form is multiplied by every step.One small change repeated thousands of times is no longer small. The point is not perfect form. It is less unnecessary cost.
