Why I built them

Training should fit the life you already have.

Most people I coach are already carrying a full life. They may have a race date, a physique goal, or both—but not hours to sort through conflicting advice.

You should not have to become your own coach before you can train well.

FORM and FORGE keep the goal, the starting point, the days available, and the work already done in view, then make the next step clear.

I built them from the systems I use with my athletes in Miami. Open the app. See today’s work. Execute.
