01 / The Practice

I develop
runners.

I look for ease.

01
Running should click.

Fluid. Connected. Almost meditative. When it doesn’t, I look for what is getting in the way.

02
Change what matters most.

The same movement can matter in one runner and not another. I change the thing that will free the most.

03
Repeat it until it belongs to you.

Ten minutes counts. A cue becomes a habit. A habit becomes the way you run.

Like a kite upon the wind.

That’s what I’m looking for. Ease. Confidence. Nothing fighting what wants to move.

02 / Coaching

Hands on when it matters.

When something stands out, it has the quality of an out-of-tune note. I try one useful change, then watch the next rep.

Not everything needs fixing.Some things just cost you more.

Pace and heart rate tell me what happened. How the work felt, what you chose when it got hard, and whether the cue held help decide what comes next.

Form is multiplied by every step.One small change repeated thousands of times is no longer small. The point is not perfect form. It is less unnecessary cost.
