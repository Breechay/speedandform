Why I built them

More information was not the answer.

People were already collecting workouts, exercises, and pacing advice from everywhere, then trying to apply all of it at once.

The advice was not always bad. It simply was not organized around their body, their goal, their schedule, or the work they had already done.

That is how training becomes busy without becoming useful.

I built FORM and FORGE from the systems I use with my athletes in Miami. They put the right work in the right place, so you can open the app and execute.

Coach Brice · Miami

In the right order.
