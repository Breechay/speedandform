Then the morning opens up. Sit with the people you ran with. Bring someone to breakfast. Read, work or put your feet up.

For the host

A good morning.
Every detail
accounted for.

A hotel, residence or performance property. One morning, planned together.

Who comes

FORM runners, the host’s guests or residents, and friends joining for breakfast. Each place is reserved in advance, including breakfast-only guests.

Keep the first morning small enough to welcome everyone personally. Set the guest limit around run support, showers, seating and the kitchen. Each runner keeps the distance and effort that fit their training.

What the place needs

An early welcome, secure belongings, clean bathrooms, changing space, showers and towels. A clear route out to the road, track or waterfront.

Afterward: water, shade, comfortable seating, good coffee and proper breakfast. Pool, garden or recovery facilities can be part of the experience where access is agreed.

Who takes care of what
FORM
The routes, run groups, participant communication and coaching. Brice leads the practice; agree a named lead for each departure.
The host
Property access, belongings, changing and recovery facilities, food, drinks and hospitality staff.
Together
Guest numbers, return windows, staffing, weather decisions and an agreed response if someone needs help. A host stays at the property while the runs are out.
Breakfast and the return

Plan the two departures to bring people back within a shared breakfast window. Water and refreshments are ready for early returns; breakfast follows as people shower and settle in.

One clear inclusion: breakfast, coffee and somewhere comfortable to stay. Agree the menu, dietary needs and service window in advance. Staying should not depend on placing another order.

Before the invitation goes out

Confirm the property, date, guest limit, routes, meeting point and finish time. Agree access, staffing, direct costs, FORM’s fee and who pays.

The invitation names what is included, any guest price, what to bring, how to reserve and what happens if the weather changes. Photography and permission to use images are agreed separately.
