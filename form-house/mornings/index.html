For the host

Make room
for the morning.

For a hotel, residence or performance property. We shape the running and the hours around it together.

Who comes

FORM runners, the property’s guests or residents, and friends joining for breakfast. Everyone reserves a place, including guests coming just for the table.

The first morning is small enough for a personal welcome. Capacity follows the run support, showers, seating and kitchen. Each runner keeps the training that fits them.

What the place needs

Early access, secure belongings, clean bathrooms, changing space, showers and towels. A good route out to the road, track or waterfront.

On return: refreshments, breakfast, shade and comfortable seats. Pool or recovery access can be included when agreed with the property.

Who takes care of what
FORM
Routes, group guidance and guest communication. Brice directs the running practice, with a named lead for each departure.
The host
The welcome at the property, belongings, changing and recovery space, food, drinks and hospitality staff.
Together
Guest numbers, timing, staffing and the weather plan. Agree who stays at the property and who responds if someone needs help.
Breakfast and the return

Two departures, planned around the same breakfast window. Water, a sodium-containing electrolyte drink and easy food are ready for the first runner back. Nobody has to wait for the full group or a shower to eat.

Offer a substantial bowl, shake or savory breakfast with carbohydrate and protein. Confirm recipes, portions, dietary substitutions and ingredient labels with the kitchen. Keep fruit, drinks and extra breakfast available across the return window.

Breakfast, coffee and a place to stay are included together. Guests can remain without placing another order.

Before the invitation goes out

Confirm the property, date, guest limit, routes, meeting point and finish time. Agree access, staffing, direct costs, FORM’s fee and who pays.

The invitation names what is included, any guest price, what to bring, how to reserve and what happens if the weather changes. Photography and permission to use images are agreed separately.
