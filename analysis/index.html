Example format · No athlete data

From observation to practice.

01 / Your question
“Easy runs feel effortful. What should I pay attention to?”
02 / What the evidence shows
Time-stamped observations from the supplied clips, considered alongside pace, fatigue and recent training. Uncertainty is stated.
03 / The useful priorities
Two or three changes to try, or features to leave alone. Each comes with a reason and a way to notice the response.
04 / Your next two weeks
A short practice outline with exposures, a check-in question and a clear point to reassess.

A few practical questions.

Know what you are choosing before you commit.

Already have a coach or training plan?

A focused outside read can sit alongside your existing coaching or plan. Tell me the question you want help with and any direction your coach has already given you. We agree on a useful scope before you pay.

Is this the free Miami running assessment?

No. The free Miami assessment is a conversation and a look at your running to establish coaching fit. FORM Analysis is a separate paid deliverable: a recorded review, written priorities, practice outline and one follow-up.

Can you diagnose pain or tell me my injury risk?

No. This is a coaching review of running and training, not a medical assessment. There is no universal ideal form, injury prediction score or promise of improved running economy.

Does it include a training plan?

It includes a short practice outline related to the review. It does not include a full race plan, ongoing weekly programming or unlimited messaging. Run Development is the ongoing coaching option.

How do I pay?

Send an inquiry first. I confirm fit, usable footage and delivery capacity, then provide the payment instructions and agreed scope. The form does not take payment or reserve a place.
