Running form & strength

Movement, cues and the strength work around your running.

  • LessonWhich running cue should I try?Simple running cues for shoulders, steps, posture and breathing. Learn when to try one, what to notice, and when to leave it alone.→
  • LessonWhat should I change in my running form?Running form explained simply: notice a real cost, try one small cue and compare. Includes cadence, heel strike, relaxed shoulders and a practice metronome.→
  • LessonStrength training for runnersFour useful movement patterns, a manageable starting dose, and room for your running.→
  • RoutineWhat strength exercises should a runner start with?Follow a simple runner strength session: squat or split squat, hinge, calf raise and trunk control. Clear steps, doses, easier versions and weekly placement.→
  • RoutineHow should I warm up before running?A simple pre-run warm-up: start gently, choose familiar movements, and build toward the day’s effort without tiring yourself first.→
  • RoutineHow do I use mobility work around running?Simple mobility options for runners, with clear setup, dose and cues for hips, ankles, calves and upper back. Choose comfortable movement and review the response.→
  • RoutineHow do I practice keeping my trunk steady?A simple anti-rotation exercise guide for runners. Learn dead bugs, Pallof presses, side planks and single-leg hinges with setup, starting dose and clear cues.→
  • LessonShould every muscle get the same amount of work?Understand strength-training volume with a simple time-budget metaphor. Prioritize a goal, maintain other areas and adjust from performance and recovery.→
  • LessonWhy does a training program have phases?Training phases explained simply: give one priority time, keep other abilities in practice and move the emphasis when the goal and response support it.→
  • LessonWhere should I start with running mechanics?A simple running mechanics map: notice rhythm, control and direction, choose one useful question, and test a change without diagnosing a runner from a video.→
