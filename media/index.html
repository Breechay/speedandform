About

Speed & Form is a coaching practice led by Brice Ikouebe. We develop runners and strength athletes through individual coaching, shared training and documented studies of the work.

FORM is the athlete team and shared practice around the coaching work. It is not a public run club.

Founder and coach

Brice Ikouebe

Brice Ikouebe is the founder and coach behind Speed & Form, a Miami-based practice for runners and strength athletes. His work combines individualized training, movement observation and repeated exposure to help athletes build capability that holds up in the real work.

FORM

The athletes inside the practice.

FORM is the group of athletes coached through Speed & Form. They train toward individual goals, race, test ideas in practice and come together for selected shared sessions and experiences.
